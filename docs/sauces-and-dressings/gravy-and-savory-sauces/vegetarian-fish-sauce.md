# Vegetarian Fish Sauce

![Vegetarian Fish Sauce](../../assets/images/vegetarian-fish-sauce.webp){ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 12 | 20 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :droplet: 3 cups (681 g) water
- :mushroom: 0.25 oz (5 g) dried sliced shiitake mushrooms
- :salt: 3 Tbsp salt
- :takeout_box: 2 Tbsp (18 g) soy sauce

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 medium saucepan
- 1 fine-mesh sieve
- 1 airtight container
- 1 refrigerator

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a medium saucepan, simmer water, dried sliced shiitake mushrooms, salt, and soy sauce over medium heat until reduced
by half (to about 1.5 cups), about 20 minutes.

### Step 2

Strain the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve into an airtight container. Allow to cool to room temperature, then seal
and store in the refrigerator for up to 3 weeks.
