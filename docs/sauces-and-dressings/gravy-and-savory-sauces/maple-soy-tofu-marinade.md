# :takeout_box: Maple Soy Tofu Marinade

![Maple Soy Tofu Marinade][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 4 | 20 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :cheese: 1 block (400 to 450 g) extra firm tofu
- :takeout_box: 1/4 cup (60 g) tamari
- :wine_glass: 3 Tbsp (45 g) rice vinegar
- :honey_pot: 1 Tbsp (20 g) maple syrup
- :olive: 1 tsp (5 g) toasted sesame oil
- :garlic: 2 large cloves garlic
- :sweet_potato: 1 Tbsp (15 g) ginger

## :cooking: Cookware

- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 large bowl
- :package: 1 airtight container
- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 medium skillet

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Cut the extra firm tofu (14 to 16-ounce) into the desired shape. In a large bowl, mix the tamari, rice vinegar, maple
syrup, toasted sesame oil (optional), minced garlic, and minced ginger. Transfer the cut tofu to the bowl and toss to
coat. Place the tofu in an airtight container and pour the remaining sauce onto the tofu. Marinate for at least 15
minutes. Or refrigerate for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months. Thaw in the refrigerator before using. Bake,
grill, air fry, or pan fry the tofu. Heat the sauce in a medium skillet over medium-low heat. Add the cornstarch slurry
(1 teaspoon cornstarch dissolved in 1 tablespoon water) to the saucepan for a thicker sauce.
