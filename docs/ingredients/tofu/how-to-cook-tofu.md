# :butter: How to Cook Tofu

![How to Cook Tofu][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 4 | 1.08 hours |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :cheese_wedge: 16 oz (454 g) extra-firm tofu
- :stew: 2 Tbsp (30 g) low-sodium [soy sauce](../soy-sauce.md)
- :garlic: 1 tsp (3 g) garlic powder
- :cheese: 2 Tbsp (8 g) nutritional yeast
- :droplet: 1 Tbsp (15 g) water
- :hot_pepper: 1.5 tsp (8 g) sriracha
- :olive: 2 tsp (9 g) olive oil
- :chestnut: 2 tsp (5 g) cornstarch

## :cooking: Cookware

- :bowl_with_spoon: bowl
- :shallow_pan_of_food: oven
- :cookie: baking sheet
- :bowl_with_spoon: air fryer
- :shallow_pan_of_food: skillet

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

If using pre-pressed super-firm or extra-firm tofu, pat dry with paper towels. Otherwise, press the tofu for 10 to 15
minutes and pat dry. Cut the extra-firm tofu into cubes, planks, or triangles. Transfer to a medium bowl.

### Step 2

Preheat the oven to 375°F.

### Step 3

Make the marinade: In a small bowl, whisk together the low-sodium [soy sauce](../soy-sauce.md) (or tamari), garlic powder, nutritional
yeast, water, sriracha (optional), olive oil (or avocado oil), and cornstarch (or arrowroot). Adjust seasoning to taste.

### Step 4

Pour the marinade over the tofu and gently toss until evenly coated.

### Step 5

Oven-Bake: Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone mat. Spread the tofu in an even layer with
space between the pieces. Bake for 25 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden brown and crispy on the outside.

### Step 6

Air-Fryer Method (Alternative): Transfer marinated tofu to the basket of an air fryer in a single layer. Air-fry at 375
°F for 10 to 15 minutes, shaking the basket halfway through, until crisp.

### Step 7

Pan-Fry Method (Alternative): Heat 1 to 2 tablespoons of oil in a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tofu
in a single layer and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, turning occasionally, until all sides are golden brown and crisp.

### Step 8

Serve warm in grain bowls, salads, veggie stir-fries, or wraps.
