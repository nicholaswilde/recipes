# :candy: Golden Syrup

![Golden Syrup][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 24 | 60 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :candy: 4 cups (792 g) cane sugar
- :droplet: 1.25 cups (284 g) water
- :tangerine: 2 Tbsp (28 g) lemon juice
- :takeout_box: 0.5 tsp citric acid

## :cooking: Cookware

- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 saucepan
- :spoon: 1 candy thermometer
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 jar

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Place cane sugar and water in a saucepan and stir to combine. Bring it to a boil, stirring regularly to prevent burning
until the sugar is dissolved. Stir very gently to prevent sugar water from splashing up the sides of the saucepan.

### Step 2

Once boiling, gently stir in lemon juice (either fresh or bottled will work, though fresh produces a better flavor) or
citric acid.

### Step 3

Reduce the heat to a very low and gentle simmer and leave the saucepan uncovered. Do not stir the syrup again. Let it
simmer on very low for 40 to 60 minutes or longer until the sugar turns a rich amber color (about 240 °F to 250 °F on
a candy thermometer).

### Step 4

Turn off the heat and let it sit for a few minutes, then pour the hot syrup into a jar and let it cool completely before
closing with a tight-sealing lid. Store at room temperature for several months.

!!! tip

    This makes approximately 3 cups of golden syrup. If your syrup cools too thick and stiff, reheat it gently while
    adding a splash of water. If your syrup is too runny, simmer it longer to caramelize further.
