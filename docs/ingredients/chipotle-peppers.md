# :hot_pepper: Chipotle Peppers

![Chipotle Peppers][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 10 | 13.00 hours |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :wood: 1 pecan wood chips or pellets
- :hot_pepper: 20 jalapeño peppers ((preferably red))

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 smoker
- 1 paring knife
- 1 wire rack
- 1 dehydrator
- 1 oven

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Set smoker temperature to 200°F. Add pecan wood chips or pellets (apple, cherry, hickory, or oak also work).

### Step 2

Wash and dry jalapeño peppers ((preferably red)). Make a slit with a paring knife from stem to tip along the side of
each pepper. Place in a single layer on a wire rack that fits inside the smoker.

### Step 3

Smoke the peppers for 3 hours. Remove from smoker. If not storing immediately in [adobo sauce](../sauces-and-dressings/gravy-and-savory-sauces/adobo-sauce.md), dry completely.

### Step 4

Dry the smoked peppers in a dehydrator at 125 °F to 135°F, or in an oven set at 170 °F to 200 °F (or its lowest
temperature) for 6 to 10 hours, until completely dried and leathery. Store in an airtight container or grind into
chipotle powder.
