# :droplet: 1947 Oregon Trail [Sourdough Starter](sourdough-starter.md)

![1947 Oregon Trail Sourdough Starter][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 1 cup | 12.08 hours |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :droplet: 1 Tbsp (14 g) lukewarm water
- :apple: 0.5 tsp (2 g) dry starter granules
- :bread: 4 tsp (8 g) flour
- :droplet: 1 tsp (5 g) water
- :droplet: 3 Tbsp (43 g) water
- :bread: 0.25 cup (23 g) flour
- :droplet: 0.33 cup (75 g) water
- :bread: 0.5 cup (46 g) flour

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 small container
- 1 larger container

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a small container, mix lukewarm water and dry starter granules. Let stand for 5 minutes to soften the starter
granules.

### Step 2

Mix in flour. If needed, add an additional water or 2 tsp until the mixture reaches the consistency of stiff pancake
batter (about a 3:4 ratio of water to flour).

### Step 3

Place the mixture in a warm place between 70 °F and 85 °F (21 °C to 29°C) for 4 to 12 hours until bubbly.

### Step 4

Transfer to a larger container. Stir in water and flour. Let sit until the mixture rises up.

### Step 5

Add water plus 1 Tbsp, and flour. Stir well and let sit until bubbly. Once bubbly, you will have about 1 cup of active
starter ready for baking or refrigerated storage.
