# :glass_of_milk: Cream Cheese

![Cream Cheese][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 8 | 20 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :glass_of_milk: 8 cups (1816 g) whole milk
- :tangerine: 5 Tbsp (70 g) lemon juice
- :salt: 1/2 tsp salt

## :cooking: Cookware

- :wastebasket: 1 fine-mesh strainer
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 cheesecloth
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 bowl
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 heavy-bottomed saucepan
- :gear: 1 food processor
- :gear: 1 blender

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Line a fine-mesh strainer with cheesecloth (or a nut milk bag) and set it over a bowl.

### Step 2

Heat the whole milk in a heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium-high heat until steaming with small bubbles at the edges
(gentle simmer). Do not boil.

### Step 3

Stir in the lemon juice (or vinegar) until curds form, and the whey begins to separate. Turn off the heat and rest 2 to
3 minutes.

### Step 4

Pour into the lined strainer and drain until the curds are cool enough to handle (reserve the whey). Optional: rinse
with cold water for a milder flavor, then drain again.

### Step 5

Gather the cloth and squeeze firmly to remove excess whey.

### Step 6

Transfer the curds to a food processor or blender, add salt, and blend until completely smooth and fluffy. If it seems
dry or slightly grainy, keep blending and drizzle in 1 to 2 tablespoons reserved whey a little at a time until creamy.

### Step 7

Taste and adjust salt, then transfer to an airtight container and chill.
