# :bread: Panko Breadcrumbs

![Panko Breadcrumbs][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 3 | 15 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :bread: 1 loaf white bread
- :herb: some dried herbs

## :cooking: Cookware

- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 oven
- :gear: 1 food processor
- :cookie: 1 baking sheet

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 300°F.

### Step 2

Remove the crusts from white bread.

### Step 3

Gently crumble the bread into coarse crumbs either with your hands or a food processor.

### Step 4

Spread crumbs onto a baking sheet and toss with dried herbs (optional), if desired.

### Step 5

Dry bread crumbs in the oven 7 to 10 minutes, stirring after the first 5 minutes.

### Step 6

Cool completely and store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place for up to 3 months.
