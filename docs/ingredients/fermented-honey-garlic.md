# Fermented Honey Garlic

![Fermented Honey Garlic][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 16 | 14 days |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :garlic: 1 cup organic garlic
- :honey_pot: 1 cup (180 g) raw unpasteurized honey
- :apple: 1 tbsp (18 g) apple cider vinegar

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 jar

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Grab a large jar (about 16-20 oz) along with its lid and wash them both with warm water and soap. Let them air dry.

### Step 2

Peel the skin off all the organic garlic (about 3-4 heads) and slice off the hard knob. Place all the garlic into the
jar, leaving about 1/2 to 1/4 of the jar empty to allow for bubbling and fermentation.

### Step 3

Pour raw unpasteurized honey over the garlic. Let it settle. If there is any exposed garlic after the honey has settled,
add up to 0.25 cup more honey.

### Step 4

Add a splash of apple cider vinegar (optional) to increase acidity if desired. Seal the jar tightly and store in a cool,
dark location (like a cupboard or pantry).

### Step 5

Every day for two weeks, turn the jar upside down and then right side up to mix and keep the garlic evenly coated in
honey.

### Step 6

Every 2 days, open the jar to burp it (release fermentation bubbles), then reseal.

### Step 7

Ferment for at least 2 weeks (up to a year). The flavor improves over time. Keep the lid tight and store in a cool, dark
place.
