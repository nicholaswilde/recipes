# :cookie: English Shortbread

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 8 | 30 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :candy: 0.25 cup (39 g) sugar
- 0.25 lb [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- 1 tsp [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)
- :bread: 1 cup (120 g) all-purpose flour
- :candy: 1 sugar

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 mixing bowl
- 1 9-inch round cake tin
- 1 fork

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 325°F.

### Step 2

In a mixing bowl, cream sugar, softened [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md), and [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md). Gradually add all-purpose flour until combined into
a dough.

### Step 3

Press mixture firmly into an ungreased 9-inch round cake tin.

### Step 4

Prick all over with a fork and flute edges like a pie crust.

### Step 5

Bake at 325 °F until lightly browned, about 30 minutes.

### Step 6

Cut into wedges while warm. Sprinkle lightly with sugar.
