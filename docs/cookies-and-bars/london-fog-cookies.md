# :cookie: London Fog Cookies

![London Fog Cookies][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:-----------------------: |
| 2.06 days |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :butter: 12 Tbsp (170 g) unsalted [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :cheese_wedge: 3 Earl Grey teabags
- :takeout_box: 0.25 tsp culinary lavender
- :candy: 0.5 cup (99 g) granulated sugar
- :candy: 0.5 cup (114 g) light [brown sugar](../ingredients/brown-sugar.md)
- 2 tsp [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)
- :salt: 0.5 tsp kosher salt
- :bread: 1.5 cups (180 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md)
- :chestnut: 0.5 tsp baking soda
- :egg: 1 large egg
- :butter: 3 Tbsp (42 g) unsalted [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :candy: 0.75 cup (150 g) powdered sugar
- :glass_of_milk: 1 Tbsp (14 g) whole milk
- 0.5 tsp [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)
- :salt: 1 salt

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 small saucepan
- 1 mixing bowl
- 1 small bowl
- 1 silicone spatula
- 1 4-tablespoon scoop
- 1 small sheet pan
- 1 large plate
- 1 large baking sheets
- 1 cooling rack
- 1 microwave-safe bowl

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a small saucepan over medium heat, add the unsalted [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md). Once the [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) is mostly melted, cut open the Earl Grey
teabags, add the tea and stir for a 1 minute. Add the culinary lavender (optional), if using, then remove the pot from
heat. Steep, covered, for 10 minutes.

### Step 2

In a mixing bowl, combine the granulated sugar plus 2 Tbsp, light [brown sugar](../ingredients/brown-sugar.md), [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md) and kosher salt. To a
small bowl, add the all-purpose flour, [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md) and baking soda and whisk to combine.

### Step 3

Pour the tea-infused [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) over the sugar. Using a silicone spatula, vigorously stir to combine. Make sure the batter
is not sizzling hot, then add the egg and vigorously stir for 1 to 2 minutes to combine while cooling down the batter.
Add the dry ingredients and mix until there are no dry flour spots. Chill the cookie dough uncovered for 10 minutes in
the fridge.

### Step 4

When ready to bake, divide the dough into eight 1/4-cup balls (a 4-tablespoon scoop is helpful here) and place on the
small sheet pan (you should have 8 balls, each weighing about 80 grams). Transfer the cookie balls to a small
parchment-lined sheet pan or a large plate and freeze for 45 minutes, uncovered, until firm to the touch.

### Step 5

Arrange racks in the upper and lower thirds of the oven. Heat the oven to 350°F. Line two large baking sheets with
parchment paper. Arrange half of the frozen cookie dough on each sheet, leaving 5 inches between each cookie.

### Step 6

Bake for about 16 minutes, until the cookies look lightly golden and puffed up, while the centers still look a bit
underbaked. Firmly knock the bottom of the pans against a hard heat-proof surface a few times to flatten the cookies.
Cool for 5 minutes, then transfer the cookies to a cooling rack to cool completely.

### Step 7

In a microwave-safe bowl, microwave the unsalted [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) for about 30 seconds, until very soft but not completely melted.
Add the powdered sugar, whole milk, [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md) and a tiny pinch of salt. Whisk until smooth.

### Step 8

Drizzle one or two thick stripes of frosting on each room-temperature cookie using a silicone spatula. Decorate with
more lavender if you like. The glaze will take a moment to set and will maintain a softer consistency, so it’s better
if the cookies are not stacked right away. Store in an airtight container or zip-close bag for up to 2 days.
