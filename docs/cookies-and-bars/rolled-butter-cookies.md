# :butter: Rolled Butter Cookies

![Rolled Butter Cookies][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 42 | 14 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- 1 cup [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :candy: 1 cup (156 g) sugar
- 2 [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md)
- 1 tsp [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)
- :bread: 2.5 cups (300 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md)
- :salt: 0.5 tsp salt

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 stand mixer
- 1 separate bowl
- 1 cookie cutters
- 1 baking sheets
- 1 wire racks

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a stand mixer, cream [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md). Gradually add sugar and beat until light and fluffy.

### Step 2

Beat in [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md) and [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md).

### Step 3

In a separate bowl, combine all-purpose flour, [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md), and salt. Blend into creamed mixture.

### Step 4

Cover and chill the dough until firm.

### Step 5

Preheat oven to 375°F.

### Step 6

Using 1/2 of dough at a time, roll out to 1/4-inch thickness on a lightly floured surface. Cut out shapes with cookie
cutters.

### Step 7

Bake on lightly greased baking sheets at 375 °F for 10 to 12 minutes.

### Step 8

Let cool 1 to 2 minutes after baking before removing to wire racks to cool completely.
