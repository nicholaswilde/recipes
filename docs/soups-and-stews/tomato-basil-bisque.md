# :tomato: Tomato Basil Bisque

![Tomato Basil Bisque][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 6 | 1.5 hours |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :olive: 1 tsp (4 g) olive oil
- :garlic: 0.25 cup (34 g) garlic
- :garlic: 0.25 cup (39 g) shallots
- :onion: 1 cup (106 g) white onion
- :carrot: 1 cup (142 g) carrots
- :salt: 1 Tbsp salt
- :salt: 1 tsp (4 g) black pepper
- :stew: 1 cup (227 g) water or [vegetable broth](../ingredients/vegetable-broth.md)
- :tomato: 4 14.5-oz cans diced tomatoes
- :herb: 0.5 oz (14 g) fresh basil
- :glass_of_milk: 1 pint heavy cream or [cashew crema](../sauces-and-dressings/dips-and-spreads/cashew-crema.md)
- :candy: 1 tsp (3 g) sugar

!!! tip "Healthy Dairy-Free Variation"

    For a healthier, lower-saturated-fat, and vegan bisque, substitute the heavy cream with an equal amount
    (1 pint / 2 cups) of [Cashew Crema](../sauces-and-dressings/dips-and-spreads/cashew-crema.md). It delivers an
    identically rich and velvety texture without separating or curdling during simmering.

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 large pot
- 1 wooden spoon
- 1 blender

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Heat a large pot over medium heat. Add a drizzle of olive oil, then sauté garlic (diced), shallots (peeled and diced),
white onion (diced), carrots (diced), salt, and black pepper until vegetables caramelize—about 5 minutes. Stir
occasionally to prevent scorching.

### Step 2

Pour in water or [vegetable broth](../ingredients/vegetable-broth.md) and scrape the bottom of the pot with a wooden spoon
to lift all browned bits. Reduce slightly, about 1 to 2 minutes.

### Step 3

Add all four cans of diced tomatoes along with chopped fresh basil, reserving some for garnish. Bring to a gentle
simmer, then reduce heat to low and cook uncovered for 20 minutes to develop flavor.

### Step 4

Stir in the heavy cream (or cashew crema) and sugar. Continue simmering on low for another 20 minutes, stirring
occasionally to prevent sticking.

### Step 5

Transfer soup in batches to a blender. Blend on high until completely smooth and velvety. Adjust seasoning with more
salt and pepper as needed.

### Step 6

Return blended soup to the pot. Warm gently if needed. Garnish with fresh basil before serving.
