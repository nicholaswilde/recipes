# :cake: Pumpkin Spice Mini Sheet Cake

![Pumpkin Spice Mini Sheet Cake][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 9 | 40 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :candy: 0.5 cup (99 g) granulated sugar
- :candy: 0.25 cup (57 g) light [brown sugar](../ingredients/brown-sugar.md)
- :olive: 0.25 cup (50 g) vegetable oil
- :melon: 7.5 oz (213 g) pumpkin puree
- :egg: 1 egg
- :flower_playing_cards: 0.5 tsp [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)
- :chestnut: 0.5 tsp [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md)
- :chestnut: 0.5 tsp baking soda
- :salt: 0.25 tsp kosher salt
- :chestnut: 1 tsp (4 g) cinnamon
- :peanuts: 0.25 tsp nutmeg
- :sweet_potato: 0.25 tsp (1 g) ground ginger
- :chestnut: 1 pinch ground cloves
- :bread: 0.88 cup (106 g) all-purpose flour
- :glass_of_milk: 4 Tbsp (56 g) unsalted [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) softened slightly
- :cheese: 6 Tbsp (85 g) cream cheese
- :candy: 2 cups (400 g) powdered sugar
- :flower_playing_cards: 0.5 tsp [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)
- :salt: 0.25 tsp kosher salt
- :glass_of_milk: 1 Tbsp (14 g) heavy cream

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 oven
- 1 8x8 baking pan
- 1 bowl
- 1 stand mixer
- 1 offset spatula

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat the oven to 350 °F and grease an 8x8 baking pan with cooking spray.

### Step 2

In a large bowl, whisk together granulated sugar, light [brown sugar](../ingredients/brown-sugar.md), vegetable oil, pumpkin puree, egg and vanilla
extract until smooth.

### Step 3

Sprinkle [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md), baking soda, kosher salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, ground ginger, and ground cloves over the pumpkin
mixture and whisk to combine thoroughly. Finally, fold in all-purpose flour until fully combined. Don't overmix to
prevent overdeveloping the gluten -- a few lumps are normal and okay!

### Step 4

Pour the batter into the prepared pan. Place on the middle rack and bake for 20 to 25 minutes, or until the top springs
back under your finger and an inserted tester comes out clean. Let cool in the pan completely before frosting.

### Step 5

In a stand mixer, add unsalted [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) softened slightly and cream cheese and mix on medium speed until softened and
creamy, about 1 minute.

### Step 6

Add powdered sugar and mix on low speed until combined. Add [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md) and kosher salt, then whip on medium-high
for 2 to 3 minutes, or until it lightens in color and is very airy. If the consistency looks thicker than you'd prefer,
add heavy cream (optional) and whip for another minute until smooth and billowy.

### Step 7

Spread frosting evenly on top of the cake, then use an offset spatula to draw lines on the top of the cake. Enjoy!
