# Skillet Caramelized Peaches

![Skillet Caramelized Peaches][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 6 | 6 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :apple: 4 each peaches
- :apple: 0.5 each small lemon
- :salt: 1 pinch salt
- :chestnut: 0.5 tsp (2 g) cinnamon
- :butter: 0.5 stick unsalted butter
- :maple_leaf: 0.5 cup (106 g) brown sugar
- :candy: 0.25 cup (53 g) bourbon or dark rum
- :apple: 0.25 cup (70 g) apple juice
- :ice_cream: some vanilla ice cream
- :apple: some chopped pecan pralines

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 skillet
- 1 bowls

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Toss peaches (halved, pitted, and diced) with small lemon (juiced), salt, and cinnamon.

### Step 2

Melt unsalted butter and brown sugar in a skillet on medium-high heat.

### Step 3

Add the peach mixture and stir occasionally until lightly caramelized, about 5 minutes.

### Step 4

Add bourbon or dark rum (or apple juice as a non-alcoholic option) and cook for an additional 1.5 minutes.

### Step 5

Scoop vanilla ice cream into bowls and spoon over the peach mixture. Top with chopped pecan pralines.
