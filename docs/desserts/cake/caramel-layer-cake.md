# :cake: Caramel Layer Cake

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 16 | 40 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :bread: 1 Tbsp (8 g) cake flour
- :tumbler_glass: 1 cup (227 g) packed light [brown sugar](../../ingredients/brown-sugar.md)
- 7 Tbsp [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :egg: 0.5 cup egg substitute
- :bread: 2 cups (240 g) cake flour
- 1 tsp [baking powder](../../ingredients/baking-powder.md)
- :chestnut: 0.5 tsp baking soda
- :salt: 0.25 tsp salt
- :glass_of_milk: 1 cup (227 g) milk
- :chestnut: 0.5 cup (106 g) dark [brown sugar](../../ingredients/brown-sugar.md)
- 3 Tbsp [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- 1 tsp [vanilla extract](../../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)
- :salt: 0.12 tsp salt
- :glass_of_milk: 14 oz%can [sweetened condensed milk](../../ingredients/sweetened-condensed-milk.md)

## :cooking: Cookware

- 2 8-inch round cake pans
- 1 large mixing bowl
- 1 mixer
- 1 separate bowl
- 1 whisk
- 1 wire rack
- 1 saucepan
- 1 serving plate

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 350°F. Coat two 8-inch round cake pans with cooking spray; line bottoms with wax paper. Lightly coat
wax paper with cooking spray; dust pans with cake flour and set aside.

### Step 2

In a large mixing bowl, place packed light [brown sugar](../../ingredients/brown-sugar.md) and softened [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md). Beat with a mixer at medium speed for 3
minutes until well blended.

### Step 3

Add egg substitute to sugar mixture; beat well.

### Step 4

In a separate bowl, combine cake flour, [baking powder](../../ingredients/baking-powder.md), baking soda, and salt, stirring well with a whisk.

### Step 5

Add flour mixture to sugar mixture alternately with milk, beginning and ending with flour mixture.

### Step 6

Spoon batter evenly into prepared pans and sharply tap pans on counter to remove air bubbles.

### Step 7

Bake at 350 °F for 25 minutes until cake tester comes out clean.

### Step 8

Cool in pans for 10 minutes on a wire rack. Remove from pans; peel off and discard wax paper. Cool cakes completely on
wire rack.

### Step 9

For frosting: In a saucepan, combine dark [brown sugar](../../ingredients/brown-sugar.md), [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md), [vanilla extract](../../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md), salt, and [sweetened condensed milk](../../ingredients/sweetened-condensed-milk.md) over
medium heat; bring to a boil. Cook for 2 minutes or until thickened, stirring constantly.

### Step 10

Place 1 cake layer on a serving plate, spread with 1/3 cup frosting. Top with remaining cake layer. Spread remaining
frosting over top and sides of cake.
