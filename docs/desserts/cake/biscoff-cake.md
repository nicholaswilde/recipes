# :cake: Biscoff Cake

![Biscoff Cake][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 12 to 16 | 2 hours |

## :salt: Ingredients

### Cake Layers

- :cookie: 18 Biscoff cookies, finely ground
- :bread: 2 cups plus 2 Tbsp (255 g) all-purpose flour
- :chestnut: 1 3/4 tsp (7 g) [baking powder](../../ingredients/baking-powder.md)
- :chestnut: 3/4 tsp (3 g) baking soda
- :salt: 3/4 tsp (4.5 g) salt
- :butter: 1 cup (226 g) unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md), room temperature
- :candy: 2/3 cup (133 g) granulated sugar
- :maple_leaf: 1 cup (220 g) [brown sugar](../../ingredients/brown-sugar.md), packed
- :egg: 4 large [eggs](../../ingredients/eggs.md), room temperature
- :baby_bottle: 2 cups (480 g) [buttermilk](../../ingredients/buttermilk.md), room temperature

### Biscoff Filling

- :butter: 1/2 cup (113 g) unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md), room temperature
- :cookie: 1 cup (250 g) Biscoff spread
- :candy: 1 cup (125 g) powdered sugar, sifted

### White Chocolate Buttercream

- :chocolate_bar: 6 oz (170 g) white chocolate, chopped
- :glass_of_milk: 1/4 cup (58 g) heavy whipping cream
- :butter: 2 cups (452 g) unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md), slightly chilled
- :candy: 6 cups (750 g) powdered sugar, sifted
- :flower_playing_cards: 1/2 tsp (2.1 g) [vanilla extract](../../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)

### White Chocolate Drip & Assembly

- :chocolate_bar: 1 cup (150 g) white chocolate chips
- :glass_of_milk: 1/3 cup (77 g) heavy whipping cream
- :cookie: 6 Biscoff cookies, divided

## :cooking: Cookware

- :shallow_pan_of_food: Three 8-inch round cake pans
- :gear: 1 food processor or blender
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 medium bowl
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 whisk
- :gear: 1 stand mixer with paddle attachment
- :spoon: 1 rubber spatula
- :wastebasket: 1 wire rack
- :bowl_with_spoon: 2 microwave-safe bowls
- :knife: 1 cake leveler or serrated knife
- :wood: 1 cake board

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat your oven to 325°F (165°C). Spray three 8-inch round cake pans with nonstick cooking spray, line the bottoms
with parchment paper, and spray again. Set aside.

### Step 2

Place 18 Biscoff cookies in a food processor or blender and pulse until finely ground into crumbs that resemble flour.

### Step 3

In a medium bowl, combine the Biscoff crumbs, all-purpose flour, [baking powder](../../ingredients/baking-powder.md),
baking soda, and salt. Whisk until well blended and set aside.

### Step 4

In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, beat room temperature unsalted
[butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md), granulated sugar, and packed [brown sugar](../../ingredients/brown-sugar.md)
on medium speed until light and fluffy. Add room temperature [eggs](../../ingredients/eggs.md), one at a time, beating
well after each addition. Continue beating for about 2 minutes until light in color and fluffy in texture.

### Step 5

With the mixer on low speed, add 1/3 of the dry ingredient mixture and mix until just combined. Slowly add half of room
temperature [buttermilk](../../ingredients/buttermilk.md) and mix until incorporated. Continue alternating the dry
ingredients and [buttermilk](../../ingredients/buttermilk.md), ending with the dry ingredients, until fully
incorporated. Scrape down the bowl with a rubber spatula and mix on low for another 20 to 30 seconds.

### Step 6

Divide batter evenly among the prepared cake pans and bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the
center comes out clean. Cool for 10 minutes in the pans on a wire rack before inverting onto cooling racks to cool
completely.

### Step 7

In the stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, beat room temperature unsalted
[butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) and Biscoff spread on medium speed until light and fluffy. Gradually add
sifted powdered sugar and beat for 2 to 3 minutes until smooth and spreadable.

### Step 8

In a microwave-safe bowl, heat 1/4 cup heavy whipping cream until hot. Pour over chopped white chocolate and stir until
smooth and silky. If needed, microwave for another 20 to 30 seconds and stir until completely melted. Set aside to cool
slightly (it should remain warm, not hot).

### Step 9

In the stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, cream slightly chilled unsalted
[butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) on medium-high speed for about 2 minutes. With the mixer running on
medium-low speed, slowly pour in the warm melted white chocolate mixture. Reduce speed to low and gradually add sifted
powdered sugar until incorporated. Add [vanilla extract](../../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md), increase speed
to medium-high, and beat for 5 minutes until light, fluffy, and silky. (If the frosting feels too soft, refrigerate for
10 to 15 minutes to firm up).

### Step 10

In a microwave-safe bowl, heat 1/3 cup heavy whipping cream in the microwave for 60 seconds until hot. Pour hot cream
over white chocolate chips and let sit for 5 minutes. Stir until smooth and glossy, then let cool slightly to room
temperature.

### Step 11

Level each cooled cake layer with a cake leveler or serrated knife. Place the first cake layer on a cake board. Spread
half of the Biscoff filling over the top. Crumble 2 Biscoff cookies into small pieces and press into the filling. Top
with the second cake layer, spread the remaining filling, and press in another 2 crumbled Biscoff cookies. Place the
final cake layer top-side down.

### Step 12

Apply a thin crumb coat of white chocolate buttercream around the top and sides of the cake. Chill in the freezer for 10
minutes to set. Frost the cake with the remaining buttercream, smooth the sides, and chill for another 10 minutes. Apply
the white chocolate drip around the top edges, letting it drip down the sides. Decorate with the remaining 2 Biscoff
cookies.
