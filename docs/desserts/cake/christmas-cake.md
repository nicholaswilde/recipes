# :cake: Christmas Cake

![Christmas Cake][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 20 | 4 hours 45 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :grapes: 300 g (10 oz) raisins
- :peach: 150 g (5 oz) dried apricots (chopped into 1/3-inch pieces)
- :tangerine: 75 g (2.5 oz) mixed peel (diced into 1/5-inch pieces)
- :cherries: 150 g (5 oz) glacé cherries (chopped into 1/3-inch pieces)
- :grapes: 180 g (6 oz) dates (diced into 1/5-inch pieces)
- :apple: 1 1/8 cups (1 cup + 2 Tbsp) apple juice (or 1/3 cup brandy + 2/3 cup + 2 Tbsp apple juice)
- :butter: 120 g (8 Tbsp) unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) (softened)
- :candy: 1.5 cups (320 g) dark [brown sugar](../../ingredients/brown-sugar.md) (packed)
- :olive: 0.5 cup (99 g) vegetable oil
- :candy: 3 Tbsp (64 g) molasses (or [golden syrup](../../ingredients/golden-syrup.md))
- :salt: 0.5 tsp salt
- :apple: 0.5 tsp ground allspice
- :chestnut: 0.5 tsp cinnamon
- :chestnut: 0.5 tsp ground nutmeg
- :chestnut: 0.5 tsp (2 g) [baking powder](../../ingredients/baking-powder.md)
- :egg: 3 large [eggs](../../ingredients/eggs.md)
- :bread: 1 2/3 cups (200 g) all-purpose flour
- :chestnut: 0.75 cup (85 g) walnuts (chopped, optional)

## :cooking: Cookware

- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 large bowl
- :gear: 1 microwave
- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 oven
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 cake pan
- :gear: 1 hand mixer
- :page_facing_up: 1 aluminum foil
- :wastebasket: 1 wire rack

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Combine the raisins, dried apricots (chopped into 1/3-inch pieces), mixed peel (diced into 1/5-inch pieces), glacé
cherries (chopped into 1/3-inch pieces), dates (diced into 1/5-inch pieces), and apple juice (or 1/3 cup brandy plus 2/3
cup plus 2 Tbsp apple juice) in a large bowl that is microwave-safe.

### Step 2

Cover and microwave on high for 1.5 minutes until hot, then stir to coat all the fruit in liquid. Cover again and set
aside for 1 hour to plump up and cool.

### Step 3

Preheat the oven to 320 °F (160°C, or 140 °C fan). Grease and line an 8- or 9-inch (21–22 cm) round cake pan (at
least 2.75 inches / 7 cm tall) with parchment paper.

### Step 4

Using a hand mixer, beat the softened unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) and packed dark [brown sugar](../../ingredients/brown-sugar.md) until smooth and creamy, about 1
minute on medium speed.

### Step 5

Add the vegetable oil and molasses (or [golden syrup](../../ingredients/golden-syrup.md)) and beat until combined. Add the salt, ground allspice, cinnamon,
ground nutmeg, and [baking powder](../../ingredients/baking-powder.md), beating until incorporated.

### Step 6

Add the [eggs](../../ingredients/eggs.md), one at a time, beating until just incorporated after each addition.

### Step 7

Stir in the all-purpose flour. When mostly incorporated, fold in the soaked fruit mixture (including all remaining
liquid in the bowl) and chopped walnuts (optional).

### Step 8

Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan, cover tightly with aluminum foil, and bake for 2.5 hours. Remove the foil
and bake for another 30 to 45 minutes, until a skewer inserted into the center comes out clean with no wet batter
attached (check at 30 minutes).

### Step 9

Remove from the oven and cool in the pan for 20 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.

### Step 10

Serve plain, dusted with powdered sugar and topped with fresh cherries, or glazed with a plain sweet white glaze.

!!! tip "Serving, Decorating & Storage"

    - **Traditional Finish:** Cover with [marzipan](../../ingredients/frosting/marzipan.md) and rolled fondant for a classic white Christmas cake.
    - **Serving:** Pairs wonderfully with homemade pouring custard.
    - **Storage:** Keeps well in an airtight container in the refrigerator for 2–3 months, or in the freezer for up to 1 year.
