# :cake: Blackberry Wine Cake

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 12 | 1.93 hours |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :chestnut: 0.5 cup (57 g) pecans
- :cake: 18.5 oz%pkg white cake mix
- :custard: 3 oz%pkg blackberry gelatin
- :olive: 0.5 cup (99 g) vegetable oil
- :wine_glass: 0.5 cup blackberry wine
- 4 [eggs](../../ingredients/eggs.md)
- 0.5 cup [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :wine_glass: 0.5 cup blackberry wine
- :candy: 2 cups (400 g) powdered sugar

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 12-cup bundt pan
- 1 mixing bowl
- 1 small saucepan
- 1 fork
- 1 serving plate

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 12-cup bundt pan. Sprinkle chopped pecans evenly over bottom of pan; set aside.

### Step 2

In a mixing bowl, blend white cake mix, blackberry gelatin, vegetable oil, blackberry wine, and [eggs](../../ingredients/eggs.md) on low speed for 1
minute.

### Step 3

Increase speed to medium-high and beat for 5 minutes. Pour batter into the prepared pan.

### Step 4

Bake for 45 to 50 minutes until a tester comes out clean.

### Step 5

While cake is baking, prepare the glaze: In a small saucepan, mix melted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) and blackberry wine. Gradually stir in
powdered sugar until smooth.

### Step 6

Remove baked cake from oven. With a fork, poke deep holes over the entire surface. Pour glaze evenly over hot cake.

### Step 7

Let cake cool in pan for about 1 hour. Invert cake onto a serving plate and serve.
