# :cake: Coffee and Walnut Layer Cake

![Coffee and Walnut Layer Cake][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 8 | 1 hour |

## :salt: Ingredients

### Sponge

- :chestnut: 50 g walnut pieces
- :candy: 225 g caster sugar
- :butter: 225 g soft unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :bread: 200 g plain flour
- :coffee: 4 tsp instant [espresso powder](../../ingredients/seasonings/espresso-powder.md)
- :chestnut: 2 1/2 tsp [baking powder](../../ingredients/baking-powder.md)
- :chestnut: 1/2 tsp baking soda
- :egg: 4 large [eggs](../../ingredients/eggs.md)
- :glass_of_milk: 1 to 2 Tbsp milk

### Buttercream Frosting

- :candy: 350 g icing sugar
- :butter: 175 g soft unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :coffee: 2 1/2 tsp instant [espresso powder](../../ingredients/seasonings/espresso-powder.md)
- :droplet: 1 Tbsp boiling water
- :chestnut: 10 walnut halves

## :cooking: Cookware

- :shallow_pan_of_food: Two 8-inch (20cm) sandwich cake pans
- :page_facing_up: Baking parchment
- :gear: Food processor
- :wastebasket: Wire rack
- :bowl_with_spoon: Cake stand

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat the oven to 180°C / 160°C fan / 350°F (gas mark 4).

### Step 2

[Butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) two 8-inch (20cm) :shallow_pan_of_food: sandwich cake pans and line the
bottom of each with :page_facing_up: baking parchment.

### Step 3

Put :chestnut: walnut pieces and :candy: caster sugar into a :gear: food processor and blitz to a fine nutty powder.

### Step 4

Add :butter: soft unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md), :bread: plain flour, :coffee: instant
[espresso powder](../../ingredients/seasonings/espresso-powder.md), :chestnut: [baking powder](../../ingredients/baking-powder.md),
:chestnut: baking soda, and :egg: large [eggs](../../ingredients/eggs.md) and process to a smooth batter.

### Step 5

Add 1 to 2 Tbsp :glass_of_milk: milk, pouring it down the funnel with the motor running or pulsing, to loosen the cake
mixture to a soft, dropping consistency. (If making by hand, bash the nuts to a rubbly powder with a rolling pin and mix
with the dry ingredients; cream the [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) and sugar together, then beat in dry
ingredients and [eggs](../../ingredients/eggs.md) alternately, followed by the milk.)

### Step 6

Divide the mixture evenly between the 2 lined pans and bake in the oven for 25 minutes, or until the sponge has risen
and feels springy to the touch.

### Step 7

Cool the cakes in their pans on a :wastebasket: wire rack for about 10 minutes, before turning them out onto the rack
and peeling off the baking parchment to cool completely.

### Step 8

To make the buttercream, pulse :candy: icing sugar in the :gear: food processor until lump-free, then add :butter: soft
unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) and process to make a smooth icing.

### Step 9

Dissolve :coffee: instant [espresso powder](../../ingredients/seasonings/espresso-powder.md) in :droplet: boiling water
and add it while still hot to the processor, pulsing to blend into the buttercream. (If making by hand, sieve the icing
sugar and beat it into the [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) with a wooden spoon, then beat in the hot coffee
liquid.)

### Step 10

Place 1 sponge upside down on a :bowl_with_spoon: cake stand or serving plate. Spread with about half the buttercream
frosting, then top with the second sponge right side up so the two flat sides meet in the middle. Cover the top with the
remaining icing in a swirly pattern.

### Step 11

Gently press :chestnut: walnut halves into the top of the icing around the edge of the circle about 1 cm apart.
