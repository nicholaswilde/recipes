# :pie: Treacle Tart

![Treacle Tart][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 8 | 1 hour 35 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :bread: 1.75 cups (210 g) all-purpose flour
- :salt: 0.5 tsp salt
- :butter: 5 Tbsp (71 g) unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :butter: 5 Tbsp (58 g) vegetable shortening
- :droplet: 0.33 cup (75 g) ice water
- :honey_pot: 1.25 cups [golden syrup](../../ingredients/golden-syrup.md)
- :tangerine: 1 lemon, zested
- :tangerine: 1 Tbsp (14 g) lemon juice
- :bread: 1.25 cups (105 g) fresh white [breadcrumbs](../../ingredients/breadcrumbs.md)
- :egg: 1 egg
- :glass_of_milk: 3 Tbsp (43 g) heavy cream

## :cooking: Cookware

- :gear: 1 food processor
- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 oven
- :wood: 1 rolling pin
- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 9-inch tart pan
- :knife: 1 fork
- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 saucepan
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 bowl

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

For the shortcrust pastry, place all-purpose flour and salt in a food processor and pulse to blend. Add cold unsalted
[butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) and cold vegetable shortening (or additional cold unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md)) and pulse a few times until the mixture
resembles coarse crumbs. Add ice water a little at a time, pulsing between additions, until the dough holds together
when pressed between your fingers without over-mixing.

### Step 2

Form the dough into a ball, flatten into a 1-inch thick disk, wrap in plastic wrap, and refrigerate for at least 30
minutes.

### Step 3

Preheat the oven to 375°F. Roll out the chilled pastry dough with a rolling pin until slightly larger than a 9-inch
tart pan with a removable bottom. Fit the pastry into the tart pan, trim the excess, and prick the bottom multiple times
with a fork.

### Step 4

Line the pastry crust with parchment paper, fill with dried beans or pie weights, and bake on the middle shelf for 10 to
15 minutes. Remove the beans and parchment paper and bake for another 5 to 10 minutes, until the crust is a very pale
golden.

### Step 5

While the crust blind-bakes, prepare the filling. Place [golden syrup](../../ingredients/golden-syrup.md), lemon zest, and lemon juice in a medium saucepan
over low heat until hot and runny. Stir in fresh white [breadcrumbs](../../ingredients/breadcrumbs.md), turn off the heat, and let sit for 5 minutes.

### Step 6

In a small bowl, lightly beat egg and heavy cream together with a fork. Pour the egg mixture into the warm syrup mixture
and stir quickly to combine.

### Step 7

Pour the filling into the par-baked tart crust and bake at 375 °F on the middle shelf for 20 to 25 minutes, until the
pastry is golden and the filling is set and nicely browned. Let cool before slicing and serving warm with clotted cream,
whipped cream, custard, or ice cream.

!!! tip

    Substituting vegetable shortening (or all [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md)) for traditional lard keeps
    the pastry lacto-ovo vegetarian while staying flaky.
