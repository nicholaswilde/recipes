# :apple: French Apple Tart

![French Apple Tart][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 6 | 2 hours 20 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :bread: 2 cups (240 g) all-purpose flour
- :salt: 0.5 tsp kosher salt
- :candy: 1 Tbsp (10 g) sugar
- :butter: 12 Tbsp (170 g) unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :droplet: 0.5 cup (114 g) ice water
- :apple: 4 Granny Smith apples
- :candy: 0.5 cup (78 g) sugar
- :butter: 4 Tbsp (56 g) unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :apple: 0.5 cup apricot jelly
- :tumbler_glass: 2 Tbsp Calvados or rum

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 food processor
- 1 oven
- 1 sheet pan
- 1 rolling pin
- 1 knife
- 1 melon baller
- 1 pastry brush
- 1 spatula

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

For the pastry, place all-purpose flour, kosher salt, and sugar in the bowl of a food processor fitted with the steel
blade. Pulse for a few seconds to combine. Add cold unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) diced
and pulse 10 to 12 times, until the [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) is
in small bits the size of peas. With the motor running, pour ice water down the feed tube and pulse just until the dough
starts to come together. Dump onto a floured board and knead quickly into a ball. Wrap in plastic wrap and refrigerate
for at least 1 hour.

### Step 2

Preheat the oven to 400°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper.

### Step 3

Roll the dough slightly larger than 10 by 14 inches using a rolling pin. Using a ruler and a small knife, trim the
edges. Place the dough on the prepared sheet pan and refrigerate while you prepare the apples.

### Step 4

Peel Granny Smith apples and cut them in half through the stem. Remove the stems and cores with a sharp knife and a
melon baller. Slice the apples crosswise into 1/4-inch thick slices. Place overlapping slices of apples diagonally down
the middle of the tart and continue making diagonal rows on both sides of the first row until the pastry is covered with
apple slices. Sprinkle with sugar and dot with cold unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) small-diced.

### Step 5

Bake for 45 to 60 minutes, until the pastry is browned and the edges of the apples start to brown. Rotate the pan once
during cooking. If the pastry puffs up in one area, cut a little slit with a knife to let the air out. The apple juices
will burn in the pan but the tart will be fine!

### Step 6

When the tart is done, heat apricot jelly together with Calvados or rum and brush the apples and the pastry completely
with the jelly mixture using a pastry brush. Loosen the tart with a metal spatula so it doesn't stick to the paper.
Allow to cool and serve warm or at room temperature.
