# :chocolate_bar: Old-Fashioned Fudge

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 16 | 10 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :candy: 2 cups (312 g) sugar
- :glass_of_milk: 0.67 cup (152 g) milk
- :chocolate_bar: 2 oz (42 g) unsweetened chocolate
- :candy: 2 Tbsp (39 g) corn syrup
- :salt: 0.25 tsp salt
- 2 Tbsp [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- 1 tsp [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)
- :peanuts: 0.5 cup (71 g) nuts

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 loaf pan
- 1 2-qt saucepan
- 1 candy thermometer

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

[Butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) a loaf pan.

### Step 2

In a 2-qt saucepan, mix sugar, milk, unsweetened chocolate, corn syrup, and salt.

### Step 3

Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until chocolate is melted and sugar is dissolved.

### Step 4

Cook, stirring occasionally, until reaching 234 °F on a candy thermometer.

### Step 5

Remove from heat; add [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md). Cool mixture to 120 °F without stirring.

### Step 6

Add [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md); beat vigorously for 5 to 10 minutes until candy is thick and no longer glossy.

### Step 7

Quickly stir in coarsely chopped nuts.

### Step 8

Spread mixture evenly in the prepared loaf pan. Cool until firm, then cut into 1-inch squares.
