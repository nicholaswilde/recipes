# :chocolate_bar: Chocolate Cherry Cupcake Bites

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 48 | 24 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :cake: 1 pkg devil's food cake mix
- 0.33 cup + 2%Tbsp [apple butter](../../ingredients/apple-butter.md)
- :droplet: 2 Tbsp (28 g) water
- 1 Tbsp [almond extract](../../ingredients/extracts/almond-extract.md)
- :cherries: 16 oz (426 g) canned dark sweet cherries in light syrup
- :egg: 0.5 cup (60 g) fat-free egg substitute

## :cooking: Cookware

- 2 24-hole mini muffin pans
- 1 medium bowl
- 1 cutting board
- 1 stand mixer
- 1 wire rack

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 350°F. Coat two 24-hole mini muffin pans with cooking spray.

### Step 2

In a medium bowl, make the frosting: Mix together 1 cup of the devil's food cake mix, 2 Tbsp of the [apple butter](../../ingredients/apple-butter.md), water,
and 2 tsp of the [almond extract](../../ingredients/extracts/almond-extract.md) until smooth; set aside.

### Step 3

Place drained canned dark sweet cherries in light syrup on paper towels and pat dry. Transfer to a cutting board and
coarsely chop; set aside.

### Step 4

In a stand mixer on slow speed, combine remaining cake mix, remaining 1/3 cup [apple butter](../../ingredients/apple-butter.md), remaining 1 tsp almond
extract, and fat-free egg substitute for 30 seconds.

### Step 5

Increase speed to medium and mix until smooth, about 2 minutes more. Gently stir in chopped cherries.

### Step 6

Fill muffin cups 3/4 full with cherry mixture.

### Step 7

Bake at 350 °F for 15 to 17 minutes until a toothpick inserted into center comes out clean.

### Step 8

Allow to cool in pans for 5 minutes; transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

### Step 9

Frost each cupcake with 1 tsp of reserved frosting.
