# :doughnut: Gulab Jamun

![Gulab Jamun][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 16 | 3.03 hours |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :droplet: 1.5 cups (340 g) water
- :candy: 1.5 cups (297 g) granulated sugar
- :seedling: 4 green cardamom pods
- :baby_bottle: 1 tsp (5 g) rose water
- :bread: 5 Tbsp (38 g) all-purpose flour (maida)
- :glass_of_milk: 1 cup milk powder
- :chestnut: 0.12 tsp (1 g) baking soda
- :baby_bottle: 1 tsp (4 g) [ghee](../ingredients/butter/ghee.md)
- :glass_of_milk: 1 Tbsp (14 g) [yogurt](../ingredients/yogurt.md) (curd)
- :glass_of_milk: 2 Tbsp (28 g) milk
- :baby_bottle: 1 Tbsp [ghee](../ingredients/butter/ghee.md) for greasing
- :baby_bottle: 2 cups [ghee](../ingredients/butter/ghee.md) or oil for deep frying
- :chestnut: 1 tsp pistachios

## :cooking: Cookware

- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 pot
- :knife: 1 fork
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 mixing bowl
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 small bowl
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 pan
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 slotted spoon

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Combine water, granulated sugar (1.25 to 1.5 cups), and 4 crushed green cardamom pods (or 1/4 tsp cardamom powder) in a
pot. Boil until the syrup turns slightly sticky, turning off the heat just before it reaches a 1-string consistency (if
it goes too far, stir in 2 Tbsp water). Stir in rose water, cover, and keep warm.

### Step 2

Fluff all-purpose flour (maida) with a fork before measuring (standard all-purpose flour works 1:1 as a substitute for
Indian maida). Sift or whisk the flour with milk powder (use full-fat milk powder, not nonfat) and baking soda in a
mixing bowl. Add [ghee](../ingredients/butter/ghee.md) (or oil) and mix well until evenly combined.

### Step 3

In a small bowl, stir together [yogurt](../ingredients/yogurt.md) (curd) (or 3/4 Tbsp lemon juice) and milk. Add
1.5 Tbsp of the wet mixture to the dry ingredients and gently bring together into a dough without over-kneading. Add
the remaining milk mixture (plus 1 to 2 Tbsp extra milk only if needed) until a smooth, slightly sticky, non-soggy
dough forms.

### Step 4

Grease your palms with [ghee](../ingredients/butter/ghee.md) for greasing (or oil) and divide the dough into 14 to 18
equal portions. Roll each portion smoothly between your palms into completely crack-free balls so they do not split
while frying; keep them covered so they do not dry out.

### Step 5

Heat [ghee](../ingredients/butter/ghee.md) or oil for deep frying in a pan over medium heat. Test with a tiny piece of
dough—it should rise slowly without browning immediately (cool the oil slightly if it rises too fast). Check that the
sugar syrup is still hot (warm gently if cooled).

### Step 6

Slide the dough balls into the medium-hot oil and fry for 1 to 2 minutes, then reduce to low heat and fry, stirring
gently for uniform color, until deep golden brown.

### Step 7

Transfer the fried jamuns with a slotted spoon directly into the hot sugar syrup. Let soak for 3 hours until soft and
plump, then garnish with chopped pistachios and serve warm or chilled.

!!! tip

    **Maida Substitute:** *Maida* is finely milled Indian refined white wheat flour. Standard US all-purpose flour
    works as a 1:1 substitute (fluff with a fork before measuring). Use full-fat milk powder rather than nonfat for best
    texture.
