# :custard: Sand Cups

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 8 | 1.12 hours |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :glass_of_milk: 2 cups (454 g) cold milk
- :custard: some vanilla instant pudding mix
- :icecream: 8 oz%tub whipped topping
- :cookie: 12 oz%pkg vanilla wafers

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 large bowl
- 1 whisk
- 8 cups

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Pour cold milk into a large bowl. Add vanilla instant pudding mix. Beat with a whisk until well blended, 1 to 2 minutes.
Let stand for 5 minutes to thicken.

### Step 2

Stir in whipped topping and half of the crushed vanilla wafers.

### Step 3

Place 1 Tbsp of crushed wafers into the bottom of individual cups.

### Step 4

Fill cups about 3/4 full with the pudding mixture. Top with remaining crushed wafers.

### Step 5

Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving.
