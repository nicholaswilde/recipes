# Ghirardelli Brownie Mix

![Ghirardelli Brownie Mix][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:-----------------------: |
| 0 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

=== "Single Batch (1 Pouch)"

    - 1 pouch Ghirardelli Brownie Mix
    - :sweat_drops: 1/3 cup water
    - :olive: 1/3 cup vegetable oil
    - :egg: 1 egg
    - :shallow_pan_of_food: 8"x8" or 9"x9" pan

=== "Double Batch (2 Pouches)"

    - 2 pouches Ghirardelli Brownie Mix
    - :sweat_drops: 2/3 cup water
    - :olive: 2/3 cup vegetable oil
    - :egg: 2 eggs
    - :shallow_pan_of_food: 13"x9" pan

=== "Triple Batch (3 Pouches)"

    - 3 pouches Ghirardelli Brownie Mix
    - :sweat_drops: 1 cup water
    - :olive: 1 cup vegetable oil
    - :egg: 3 eggs
    - :shallow_pan_of_food: 16"x12" pan

=== "Mega Batch (6 Pouches)"

    - 6 pouches Ghirardelli Brownie Mix
    - :sweat_drops: 2 cups water
    - :olive: 2 cups vegetable oil
    - :egg: 6 eggs
    - :shallow_pan_of_food: Full-sheet pan

## :cooking: Cookware

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 325°F. Prepare baking pan by lightly greasing or spraying with non-stick cooking spray.

### Step 2

Place water, vegetable oil and egg in medium bowl and stir until fully mixed. Add brownie mix and stir until well
blended. Spread in prepared pan.

### Step 3

Bake as directed. For glass pan, add 5 minutes to the bake time. Brownies are ready to remove from oven when center is
set and edges will have begun to pull from sides of pan. Cool completely in pan before cutting.

### Step 4

Store in tightly covered container.
