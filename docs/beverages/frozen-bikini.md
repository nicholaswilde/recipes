# :clinking_glasses: Frozen Bikini

![Frozen Bikini][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 1 | 0 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :tangerine: 2 oz vodka
- :peach: 1 oz peach schnapps
- :tangerine: 2 oz (56 g) orange juice
- :peach: 3 oz peach nectar
- :tangerine: 1 splash lemon juice
- :ice_cube: 4 oz (71 g) crushed ice
- :champagne: 1 oz champagne

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 blender
- 1 goblet

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a blender, combine vodka, peach schnapps, orange juice, peach nectar, a splash of lemon juice, and crushed ice.

### Step 2

Blend until smooth and slushy.

### Step 3

Pour into a chilled goblet and top with chilled champagne.
