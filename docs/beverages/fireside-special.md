# :coffee: Fireside Special

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 1 | 0 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :tumbler_glass: 0.75 oz (10 g) Irish cream liqueur
- :tumbler_glass: 0.25 oz Amaretto
- :coffee: 5 oz (53 g) hot strong coffee
- :glass_of_milk: 1 whipped cream
- :tumbler_glass: 1 tsp Amaretto

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 mug

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a warmed mug, combine Irish cream liqueur, Amaretto, and hot strong coffee.

### Step 2

Top with a generous dollop of whipped cream and drizzle with additional Amaretto.
