# Cucumber Chia Limeade

![Cucumber Chia Limeade][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 6 | 0 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :cucumber: 2 each English or hot house cucumbers
- :apple: some agave
- :droplet: 1 cup (227 g) water
- :tangerine: 0.5 cup fresh lime juice
- :droplet: 5 cups (1135 g) water
- :chestnut: 2 Tbsp (18 g) chia seeds
- :takeout_box: 1 ice
- :tangerine: some limes

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 blender
- 1 pitcher

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Dice English or hot house cucumbers into large chunks. Reserve some for garnish.

### Step 2

In a blender, add the cucumbers, agave, and one cup of water. Blend until smooth. Transfer to a pitcher.

### Step 3

Stir in fresh lime juice (about 4 limes), five cups of water, and chia seeds.

### Step 4

Pour over ice and garnish with the reserved cucumbers and limes.
