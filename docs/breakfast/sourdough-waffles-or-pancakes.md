# :waffle: Sourdough Waffles or Pancakes

![Sourdough Waffles or Pancakes][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 4 | 12 hours 16 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :tea: 200 g (2/3 cup) [sourdough starter](../ingredients/sourdough-starter.md), stirred down discard
- :baby_bottle: 1 cup (227 g) [buttermilk](../ingredients/buttermilk.md) (or milk)
- :candy: 3 Tbsp (38 g) granulated sugar (or honey)
- :flower_playing_cards: 1 tsp (5 g) [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)
- :butter: 4 Tbsp (57 g) [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md), melted
- :bread: 2 cups (240 g) all-purpose flour
- :egg: 2 large eggs, lightly beaten
- :chestnut: 1 tsp (6 g) baking soda
- :chestnut: 1 tsp (4 g) [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md)
- :salt: 1 tsp fine sea salt

## :cooking: Cookware

- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 mixing bowl
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 whisk
- :waffle: 1 waffle iron
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 griddle

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a large mixing bowl, whisk together sourdough starter, buttermilk, granulated sugar, vanilla extract, melted butter,
and all-purpose flour until well combined. Cover the bowl and let rest on the counter at room temperature overnight, or
up to 12 hours.

### Step 2

When ready to cook, add lightly beaten eggs, baking soda, baking powder, and fine sea salt to the batter, stir to
combine, and cover the bowl. Let the batter rest for 20 minutes at room temperature while the waffle iron or griddle
preheats. (To cook the batter the same day without an overnight ferment, whisk all ingredients together in a large bowl
and let rest for 20 minutes before cooking.)

### Step 3

For waffles, preheat a waffle iron and lightly grease with cooking oil spray. Pour 3/4 cup of batter into the center of
the iron, gently spread the batter, and cook until golden brown or according to the manufacturer's directions, 3 to 6
minutes. Makes 4 standard-sized waffles.

### Step 4

For pancakes, preheat a griddle until very hot. Lightly grease the griddle and pour 1/4 cup of batter in a round shape
onto the griddle. Cook until the bottom is golden brown and bubbles cover the top of the pancake, then flip and cook
until cooked through and golden brown on both sides, about 3 minutes per side. Makes about 12 pancakes.

!!! tip

    Store cooked waffles or pancakes in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week, or freeze for up to
    3 months.
