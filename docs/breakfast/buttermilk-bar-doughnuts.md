# :doughnut: Buttermilk Bar Doughnuts

![Buttermilk Bar Doughnuts][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 16 | 50 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :bread: 3.5 cups (420 g) all-purpose flour
- :candy: 1 cup (198 g) sugar
- :chestnut: 2 tsp [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md)
- :chestnut: 0.5 tsp baking soda
- :apple: 1 tsp nutmeg
- :salt: 1 tsp salt
- :icecream: 0.75 cup (180 ml) [buttermilk](../ingredients/buttermilk.md)
- :butter: 4 Tbsp (57 g) [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :egg: 2 [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md)
- :egg: 1 egg yolk
- :flower_playing_cards: 1 tsp [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)
- :butter: 6 cups (1104 g) vegetable shortening (or vegetable oil)
- :candy: 2 cups (240 g) powdered sugar
- :glass_of_milk: 3 Tbsp whole milk
- :butter: 1 Tbsp (14 g) [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :flower_playing_cards: 1 tsp [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 stand mixer
- 1 2-cup liquid measuring cup
- 1 rubber spatula
- 1 large Dutch oven
- 1 rimmed baking sheet
- 1 parchment paper
- 1 rolling pin
- 1 bench knife or pizza cutter
- 1 paring knife
- 1 medium bowl
- 1 wire rack
- 1 paper towels
- 1 spider skimmer

## :pencil: Instructions

!!! tip

    Traditional buttermilk bar doughnuts are shaped into rectangles and scored with a shallow lengthwise slit down the
    center so they split into their iconic craggly crest as they fry. Dipping the warm bars into a vanilla powdered
    sugar glaze allows the glaze to pool inside the ridges.

### Step 1

In the bowl of a stand mixer, stir together 1 cup all-purpose flour, sugar,
[baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md), baking soda, nutmeg, and salt. In a 2-cup liquid measuring cup, whisk
together [buttermilk](../ingredients/buttermilk.md), melted [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md),
[eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md), egg yolk, and [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md).

### Step 2

Fit the stand mixer with the paddle attachment. Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients and mix on medium speed
until smooth, about 30 seconds. Decrease speed to low, add the remaining 2.5 cups all-purpose flour, and mix until just
combined, about 30 seconds. Stir the batter once or twice with a rubber spatula to ensure all flour is incorporated.
(The dough will be moist and tacky, between a cake batter and cookie dough).

### Step 3

Heat vegetable shortening in a large Dutch oven over medium-low heat until it reaches 375°F. While the oil is heating,
line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper and lightly dust it with flour.

### Step 4

Turn the dough onto a well-floured work surface. Roll the dough with a heavily floured rolling pin into a rectangle
about 1/2-inch thick (approx 7 by 14 inches). Using a bench knife or pizza cutter, cut the dough into rectangular bars
about 3.5 inches long by 1.5 inches wide, yielding 14 to 16 bars. Using a small paring knife, make a shallow lengthwise
slit (about 1/4-inch deep) down the center of each bar. Transfer the cut bars to the prepared baking sheet.

### Step 5

In a medium bowl, whisk together powdered sugar, whole milk, melted [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md), and
[vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md) until smooth to make the glaze. Cover the bowl with
plastic wrap until ready to glaze.

### Step 6

Set a wire rack in a second rimmed baking sheet and line half with a triple layer of paper towels. Using your hands or a
spider skimmer, carefully place the dough bars into the hot fat 3 or 4 at a time, adjusting the heat as necessary to
maintain oil temperature between 360°F and 365°F. Fry the doughnuts until golden brown, 60 to 75 seconds per side.
Transfer to the paper towels to drain for 1 minute.

### Step 7

While still warm, dip the top craggy side of each doughnut into the glaze, letting excess drip back into the bowl.
Transfer to the wire rack, glazed side up, to let the glaze set for 5 minutes. Serve warm.
