# :pancakes: Pumpkin Pancakes

![Pumpkin Pancakes][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 4 | 20 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :bread: 2 cups (224 g) [biscuit mix](../ingredients/bisquick.md)
- :candy: 2 Tbsp (28 g) packed light [brown sugar](../ingredients/brown-sugar.md)
- :chestnut: 2 tsp (6 g) ground cinnamon
- :apple: 1 tsp (3 g) ground allspice
- :glass_of_milk: 1.5 cups (366 g) [evaporated milk](../ingredients/evaporated-milk.md)
- :melon: 0.5 cup (114 g) pumpkin puree
- :olive: 2 Tbsp (25 g) vegetable oil
- :egg: 2 [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md)
- :flower_playing_cards: 1 tsp [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)

## :cooking: Cookware

- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 large mixing bowl
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 griddle

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a large mixing bowl, combine [biscuit mix](../ingredients/bisquick.md), packed light [brown sugar](../ingredients/brown-sugar.md), ground cinnamon, and ground allspice.

### Step 2

Add [evaporated milk](../ingredients/evaporated-milk.md), pumpkin puree, vegetable oil, [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md), and [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md). Beat until smooth.

### Step 3

Pour 1/4 to 1/2 cup batter onto a heated and lightly greased griddle. Cook until top surface is bubbly and edges are
dry, 2 to 3 minutes. Flip and cook until golden brown on the other side, 1 to 2 minutes.
