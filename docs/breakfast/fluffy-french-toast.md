# :bread: Fluffy French Toast

![Fluffy French Toast][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 6 | 30 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :bread: 1/4 cup (30 g) all-purpose flour
- :glass_of_milk: 1 cup (240 g) milk
- :egg: 3 large [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md)
- :candy: 1 Tbsp (12 g) granulated sugar
- :flower_playing_cards: 1 tsp [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)
- :chestnut: 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- :salt: 1 pinch salt
- :bread: 12 thick bread slices

## :cooking: Cookware

- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 large mixing bowl
- 1 skillet or griddle

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Measure all-purpose flour into a large mixing bowl. Slowly whisk in milk until smooth and free of lumps.

### Step 2

Whisk in large [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md), granulated sugar, [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md),
ground cinnamon, and salt until well combined and slightly frothy.

### Step 3

Heat a lightly oiled or buttered skillet or griddle over medium heat.

### Step 4

Dip thick bread slices into the milk and egg mixture, turning to ensure both sides are thoroughly saturated.

### Step 5

Working in batches, place the soaked bread slices onto the preheated skillet or griddle. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes on
each side until golden brown and cooked through. Serve warm with [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) and
maple syrup.
