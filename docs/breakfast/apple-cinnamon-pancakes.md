# :apple: Apple Cinnamon Pancakes

![Apple Cinnamon Pancakes][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 4 | 3 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :pancakes: 0.75 cup (90 g) buckwheat pancake mix
- :droplet: 0.33 cup (75 g) water
- :apple: 1 medium apple
- :chestnut: 1 tsp (3 g) ground cinnamon
- :honey_pot: 3 Tbsp (63 g) pancake syrup
- :apple: 1 cup (140 g) cinnamon applesauce

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 mixing bowl
- 1 skillet

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a mixing bowl, stir together dry buckwheat pancake mix, water, peeled, cored, and grated apple, and ground cinnamon.

### Step 2

Coat a skillet with cooking spray and heat over medium heat.

### Step 3

Ladle out batter in 2-3 Tbsp batches. Cook until browned on both sides, 2 to 3 minutes per side.

### Step 4

Top with pancake syrup and sweetened cinnamon applesauce and serve.
