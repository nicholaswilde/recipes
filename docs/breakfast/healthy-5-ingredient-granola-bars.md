# Healthy 5-Ingredient Granola Bars

![Healthy 5-Ingredient Granola Bars][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:------------------------:|
| 10 | 36 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :date: 1 heaping cup packed dates, pitted (deglet noor or medjool)
- :maple_leaf: ¼ cup (84 g) maple syrup or agave nectar (or honey if not vegan)
- :peanuts: ¼ cup (68 g) creamy salted natural peanut butter or almond butter
- :chestnut: 1 cup roasted unsalted almonds, loosely chopped
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1½ cups (135 g) rolled oats (gluten-free for GF eaters)
- :chocolate_bar: Optional additions: chocolate chips, dried fruit, banana chips, vanilla, etc.

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 food processor
- 1 large mixing bowl
- 1 small saucepan
- 1 8x8-inch baking dish or loaf pan

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Process dates in a food processor until small bits remain (about 1 minute). It should form a "dough"-like consistency.

### Step 2

Optional: Toast oats (and almonds if raw) in a 350°F (176°C) oven for 10–15 minutes or until slightly golden brown.

### Step 3

Place oats, almonds, and dates in a large mixing bowl. Set aside.

### Step 4

Warm maple syrup and peanut butter in a small saucepan over low heat. Stir and pour over the oat mixture, breaking up dates to disperse throughout.

### Step 5

Transfer to an 8x8-inch baking dish (or loaf pan for thicker bars) lined with plastic wrap or parchment paper. Press down firmly until uniformly flat — use the bottom of a glass to pack tightly so bars hold together.

### Step 6

Cover and let firm up in the fridge or freezer for 15–20 minutes.

### Step 7

Remove from pan and cut into 10 bars (or 9 squares). Store in an airtight container for up to a few days, or freeze for longer freshness.
