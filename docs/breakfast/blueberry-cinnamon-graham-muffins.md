# :cupcake: Blueberry-Cinnamon Graham Muffins

![Blueberry-Cinnamon Graham Muffins][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 12 | 20 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :cookie: 18 low-fat cinnamon grahams
- :candy: 0.25 cup (39 g) sugar
- 1 Tbsp [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md)
- :egg: 1 egg
- :glass_of_milk: 1 cup (227 g) fat-free milk
- :apple: 0.67 cup (105 g) blueberries

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 muffin tins
- 1 mixing bowl
- 1 separate bowl

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 375°F. Line or grease muffin tins.

### Step 2

In a mixing bowl, combine finely crushed low-fat cinnamon grahams, sugar, and [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md); set aside.

### Step 3

In a separate bowl, beat egg and fat-free milk.

### Step 4

Add egg mixture to graham mixture and stir until just moistened. Gently fold in blueberries (if using frozen
blueberries, do not thaw).

### Step 5

Spoon batter into muffin tins, filling about 2/3 full.

### Step 6

Bake for 18 to 20 minutes until golden and firm to the touch.
