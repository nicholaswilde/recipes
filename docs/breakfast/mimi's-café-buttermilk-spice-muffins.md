# :cupcake: Mimi's Café Buttermilk Spice Muffins

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 12 | 26 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :candy: 1 cup (156 g) sugar
- 0.5 cup [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- 3 [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md)
- :bread: 2.5 cups (300 g) all-purpose flour
- :chestnut: 2 tsp baking soda
- :chestnut: 1 tsp (3 g) ground nutmeg
- :chestnut: 0.5 tsp (2 g) ground cinnamon
- :icecream: 0.75 cup + 1%Tbsp [buttermilk](../ingredients/buttermilk.md)
- :candy: 0.5 cup (78 g) sugar
- :chestnut: 1 cup (128 g) walnuts
- :chestnut: 0.5 tsp (2 g) ground cinnamon
- :chestnut: 0.5 tsp (2 g) ground nutmeg

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 muffin tins
- 1 mixing bowl
- 1 separate bowl
- 1 small bowl

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease muffin tins with [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md).

### Step 2

In a mixing bowl, cream sugar and softened [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md). Add [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md) and beat for 1 minute.

### Step 3

In a separate bowl, sift together all-purpose flour, baking soda, ground nutmeg, and ground cinnamon.

### Step 4

Add the flour mixture and [buttermilk](../ingredients/buttermilk.md) alternately to the creamed mixture and mix until smooth.

### Step 5

In a small bowl, make the nut topping: mix sugar, finely chopped walnuts, ground cinnamon, and ground nutmeg.

### Step 6

Divide batter among greased muffin cups, filling about 3/4 full. Top each muffin with a rounded tablespoon of the nut
topping.

### Step 7

Bake at 375 °F for 20 to 25 minutes until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into center comes out clean.
