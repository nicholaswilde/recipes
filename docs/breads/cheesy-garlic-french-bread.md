# :garlic: Cheesy Garlic French Bread

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 8 | 5 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :cheese: 1 cup (113 g) Monterey Jack cheese
- :cheese: 1 cup (227 g) Muenster cheese
- :butter: 0.5 cup soft margarine
- :cheese: 0.5 cup (50 g) Parmesan cheese
- :herb: 2 Tbsp (18 g) dried parsley
- :garlic: 0.5 tsp garlic powder
- :candy: 1 tsp paprika
- :bread: 1 sourdough bread loaf
- :candy: 1 paprika

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 bowl

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a bowl, mix together shredded Monterey Jack cheese, shredded Muenster cheese, soft margarine, grated Parmesan cheese,
dried parsley, garlic powder, and paprika.

### Step 2

Liberally spread cheese mixture on each half of a cut sourdough bread loaf.

### Step 3

Sprinkle additional paprika on top.

### Step 4

Broil on the farthest shelf from heat until cheese has melted, about 5 minutes.

### Step 5

Let set slightly, then slice into pieces.
