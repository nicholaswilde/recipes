# :bread: Grilled Ranch Bread

![Grilled Ranch Bread][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 8 | 5 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- 0.5 cup [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :takeout_box: 1 packet ranch salad dressing mix
- :bread: 2 [French bread](french-bread.md) loaves

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 small bowl
- 1 broiler

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a small bowl, combine softened [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) with ranch salad dressing mix.

### Step 2

Slice [French bread](french-bread.md) loaves lengthwise and spread tops with the [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) mixture.

### Step 3

Place bread under the broiler until crispy and golden brown, about 3 to 5 minutes.
