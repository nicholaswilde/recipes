# :bread: Amish Cinnamon Bread

![Amish Cinnamon Bread][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:------------------------:|
| 8 or 16 (1 or 2 loaves) | 2 hours 25 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

=== "Original (2 Loaves)"

    ### Batter

    - :bread: 3.75 cups (450 g) all-purpose flour
    - :candy: 3 cups (594 g) granulated sugar
    - :chestnut: 1 Tbsp ground cinnamon
    - :chestnut: 1.5 tsp (6 g) [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md)
    - :chestnut: 0.75 tsp (4 g) baking soda
    - :salt: 0.75 tsp salt
    - :egg: 3 large [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md)
    - :glass_of_milk: 1.75 cups (397 g) whole milk
    - :olive: 1.33 cups (264 g) vegetable oil
    - :flower_playing_cards: 2 tsp (9 g) [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)

    #### :glass_of_milk: Milk Alternate (instead of whole milk)

    - **Powdered whole milk and water:** :glass_of_milk: 0.5 cup (50 g) powdered whole milk and
      :droplet: 1.5 cups plus 1.5 tsp (347 g) water

    ### Pan Prep & Cinnamon-Sugar Crust

    - :olive: 2 tsp (8 g) vegetable oil (1 tsp per pan; do not use spray)
    - :candy: 0.5 cup (99 g) granulated sugar
    - :chestnut: 1 tsp ground cinnamon

=== "Original — 1/2 Batch (1 Loaf)"

    ### Batter

    - :bread: 1.75 cups + 2 Tbsp (225 g) all-purpose flour
    - :candy: 1.5 cups (297 g) granulated sugar
    - :chestnut: 1.5 tsp ground cinnamon
    - :chestnut: 0.75 tsp (3 g) [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md)
    - :chestnut: 3/8 tsp (1/4 tsp + 1/8 tsp, 2 g) baking soda
    - :salt: 3/8 tsp (1/4 tsp + 1/8 tsp) salt
    - :egg: 1.5 large [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md) (1 whole egg + 1 egg yolk, or 75 g beaten egg)
    - :glass_of_milk: 0.75 cup + 2 Tbsp (199 g) whole milk
    - :olive: 0.67 cup (132 g) vegetable oil
    - :flower_playing_cards: 1 tsp (5 g) [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md)

    #### :glass_of_milk: Milk Alternate (instead of whole milk)

    - **Powdered whole milk and water:** :glass_of_milk: 0.25 cup (25 g) powdered whole milk and
      :droplet: 0.75 cup plus 1 tsp (174 g) water

    ### Pan Prep & Cinnamon-Sugar Crust

    - :olive: 1 tsp (4 g) vegetable oil (do not use spray)
    - :candy: 0.25 cup (50 g) granulated sugar
    - :chestnut: 0.5 tsp ground cinnamon

## :cooking: Cookware

- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 oven
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 clean glass jar (for sourdough variation)
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 large bowl
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 medium bowl
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 small bowl
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 whisk
- :paintbrush: 1 pastry brush
- :cookie: 1 to 2 8.5 x 4.5-inch loaf pans
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 paring knife
- :wastebasket: 1 wire rack
- :knife: 1 serrated knife

## :pencil: Instructions

=== "Original (2 Loaves)"

    ### Step 1

    Preheat the oven to 325°F.

    ### Step 2

    In a large bowl, whisk together 3.75 cups (450 g) all-purpose flour, 3 cups (594 g) granulated sugar, 1 Tbsp ground
    cinnamon, 1.5 tsp [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md), 0.75 tsp baking soda, and 0.75 tsp salt.

    ### Step 3

    In a medium bowl, lightly beat 3 large [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md). Whisk in 1.75 cups (397 g) whole milk,
    1.33 cups (264 g) vegetable oil, and 2 tsp [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md) until
    combined.

    !!! tip "Powdered whole milk alternate"

        Whisk 0.5 cup (50 g) powdered whole milk directly into the dry ingredients in Step 2 so it disperses without
        clumping, then whisk 1.5 cups plus 1.5 tsp (347 g) water into the eggs, oil, and vanilla in place of the whole
        milk.

    ### Step 4

    Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir gently until just combined, scraping the bottom of the
    bowl. A few streaks or small lumps are fine; do not overmix. Set the batter aside briefly while preparing the pans.

    ### Step 5

    Using a pastry brush, brush the bottoms and sides of two 8.5 x 4.5-inch loaf pans evenly with 2 tsp vegetable oil
    (about 1 tsp per pan). Do not use vegetable oil spray, as the loaves will stick.

    ### Step 6

    In a small bowl, stir together 0.5 cup (99 g) granulated sugar and 1 tsp ground cinnamon. Add 2 Tbsp of the
    [cinnamon-sugar](../ingredients/seasonings/cinnamon-sugar.md) mixture to each greased pan, tilting and tapping to
    coat the bottoms and sides evenly. Reserve the remaining mixture.

    ### Step 7

    Divide the batter evenly between the two prepared loaf pans. Sprinkle the remaining
    [cinnamon-sugar](../ingredients/seasonings/cinnamon-sugar.md) mixture (about 2 Tbsp per loaf) evenly over the tops.

    ### Step 8

    Bake at 325°F for 65 to 70 minutes, until a paring knife inserted into the center of each loaf comes out mostly
    clean with just a crumb or two.

    ### Step 9

    Transfer the pans to a wire rack and let the loaves cool in the pans for at least 1 hour so the crust sets and pulls
    slightly away from the edges.

    ### Step 10

    Run a paring knife tightly along the sides of each pan to release the bread, gently tilt each loaf out, and turn
    right-side up. Slice with a serrated knife to preserve the crunchy
    [cinnamon-sugar](../ingredients/seasonings/cinnamon-sugar.md) crust.

=== "Original — 1/2 Batch (1 Loaf)"

    ### Step 1

    Preheat the oven to 325°F.

    ### Step 2

    In a large bowl, whisk together 1.75 cups + 2 Tbsp (225 g) all-purpose flour, 1.5 cups (297 g) granulated sugar,
    1.5 tsp ground cinnamon, 0.75 tsp [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md), 3/8 tsp baking soda, and
    3/8 tsp salt.

    ### Step 3

    In a medium bowl, lightly beat 1.5 large [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md) (1 whole egg + 1 egg yolk, or 75 g beaten
    egg). Whisk in 0.75 cup + 2 Tbsp (199 g) whole milk, 0.67 cup (132 g) vegetable oil, and 1 tsp
    [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md) until combined.

    !!! tip "Powdered whole milk alternate"

        Whisk 0.25 cup (25 g) powdered whole milk directly into the dry ingredients in Step 2 so it disperses without
        clumping, then whisk 0.75 cup plus 1 tsp (174 g) water into the eggs, oil, and vanilla in place of the whole
        milk.

    ### Step 4

    Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir gently until just combined, scraping the bottom of the
    bowl. A few streaks or small lumps are fine; do not overmix. Set the batter aside briefly while preparing the pan.

    ### Step 5

    Using a pastry brush, brush the bottom and sides of one 8.5 x 4.5-inch loaf pan evenly with 1 tsp vegetable oil. Do
    not use vegetable oil spray, as the loaf will stick.

    ### Step 6

    In a small bowl, stir together 0.25 cup (50 g) granulated sugar and 0.5 tsp ground cinnamon. Add 2 Tbsp of the
    [cinnamon-sugar](../ingredients/seasonings/cinnamon-sugar.md) mixture to the greased pan, tilting and tapping to
    coat the bottom and sides evenly. Reserve the remaining mixture.

    ### Step 7

    Scrape the batter into the prepared loaf pan. Sprinkle the remaining
    [cinnamon-sugar](../ingredients/seasonings/cinnamon-sugar.md) mixture (about 2 Tbsp) evenly over the top.

    ### Step 8

    Bake at 325°F for 60 to 70 minutes (start checking at 60 minutes), until a paring knife inserted into the center of
    the loaf comes out mostly clean with just a crumb or two.

    ### Step 9

    Transfer the pan to a wire rack and let the loaf cool in the pan for at least 1 hour so the crust sets and pulls
    slightly away from the edges.

    ### Step 10

    Run a paring knife tightly along the sides of the pan to release the bread, gently tilt the loaf out, and turn
    right-side up. Slice with a serrated knife to preserve the crunchy
    [cinnamon-sugar](../ingredients/seasonings/cinnamon-sugar.md) crust.

=== "Sourdough (2 Loaves)"

    ### Step 1 (Night Before — Build Sweet Starter)

    In a clean glass jar, stir together 25 g active [sourdough starter](../ingredients/sourdough-starter.md) (100%
    hydration), 100 g all-purpose flour, 100 g room-temperature whole milk (70–75°F), and 50 g granulated sugar (or
    30 g for a milder sweet levain) until the sugar dissolves and no dry flour remains. Cover loosely and ferment at
    room temperature for 8 to 12 hours until doubled and bubbly. This yields about 275 g (about 1 cup; ~255 g with
    30 g sugar), ready to go straight into a single bake batch with no extra sweet starter left over.

    !!! tip "Powdered whole milk alternate (starter)"

        Whisk 2 Tbsp (13 g) powdered whole milk into the flour and sugar (or dissolve it in 6 Tbsp / 87 g
        room-temperature water at 70–75°F first) before stirring in the active sourdough starter so no powder clumps
        remain.

    ### Step 2

    The next day, preheat the oven to 325°F.

    ### Step 3

    In a large bowl, whisk together 2.75 cups + 1 Tbsp (338 g) all-purpose flour, 2.75 cups (545 g) granulated sugar,
    1 Tbsp ground cinnamon, 1.5 tsp [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md), 0.75 tsp baking soda, and
    0.75 tsp salt.

    ### Step 4

    In a medium bowl, lightly beat 3 large [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md). Whisk in the entire jar of overnight sweet
    starter (~275 g / 1 cup), 1.25 cups (284 g) whole milk, 1.33 cups (264 g) vegetable oil, and 2 tsp
    [vanilla extract](../ingredients/extracts/vanilla-extract.md) until smooth and combined.

    !!! tip "Powdered whole milk alternate (batter)"

        Whisk 6 Tbsp (37 g) powdered whole milk directly into the dry ingredients in Step 3 so it disperses smoothly,
        then whisk 1 cup plus 1.5 Tbsp (247 g) water into the wet mixture in place of the whole milk.

    ### Step 5

    Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir gently until just combined, scraping the bottom of the
    bowl. A few streaks or small lumps are fine; do not overmix. Set the batter aside briefly while preparing the pans.

    ### Step 6

    Using a pastry brush, brush the bottoms and sides of two 8.5 x 4.5-inch loaf pans evenly with 2 tsp vegetable oil
    (about 1 tsp per pan). Do not use vegetable oil spray, as the loaves will stick.

    ### Step 7

    In a small bowl, stir together 0.5 cup (99 g) granulated sugar and 1 tsp ground cinnamon. Add 2 Tbsp of the
    [cinnamon-sugar](../ingredients/seasonings/cinnamon-sugar.md) mixture to each greased pan, tilting and tapping to
    coat the bottoms and sides evenly. Reserve the remaining mixture.

    ### Step 8

    Divide the batter evenly between the two prepared loaf pans. Sprinkle the remaining
    [cinnamon-sugar](../ingredients/seasonings/cinnamon-sugar.md) mixture (about 2 Tbsp per loaf) evenly over the tops.

    ### Step 9

    Bake at 325°F for 65 to 70 minutes, until a paring knife inserted into the center of each loaf comes out mostly
    clean with just a crumb or two.

    ### Step 10

    Transfer the pans to a wire rack and let the loaves cool in the pans for at least 1 hour so the crust sets and pulls
    slightly away from the edges.

    ### Step 11

    Run a paring knife tightly along the sides of each pan to release the bread, gently tilt each loaf out, and turn
    right-side up. Slice with a serrated knife to preserve the crunchy
    [cinnamon-sugar](../ingredients/seasonings/cinnamon-sugar.md) crust.

!!! tip "Notes & Conversion Details"

    - **Single-Bake Sourdough Conversion:** The 2-loaf overnight sweet starter (~275 g / ~1 cup, made with 50 g sugar
      for a sweeter profile) contributes 112.5 g flour, 112.5 g liquid (100 g milk + 12.5 g starter water), and 50 g
      sugar. The 1-loaf starter (~138 g / ~1/2 cup) contributes half those amounts (56.5 g flour, 56.5 g liquid, 25 g
      sugar). Subtracting these from the main batter preserves identical hydration and crumb without leaving five extra
      cups of sweet starter on hand.
    - **Half-Batch Egg & Bake Time:** Halving 3 large eggs works cleanly using 1 whole large egg plus 1 egg yolk (for
      rich, custardy crumb) or 1 whole egg plus half of a beaten egg (~75 g total). Start checking a single loaf for
      doneness at 60 minutes.
    - **Powdered Whole Milk Ratio:** Reconstitutions use standard whole milk solids composition (~12.5% powdered whole
      milk and ~87.5% water by weight, matching the `0.5 cup = 50 g` dried whole milk density in the
      [measuring reference](../reference/measuring.md)).
    - **No Pudding Mix:** This recipe skips the boxed vanilla pudding mix found in many modern versions, relying on eggs
      and whole milk for a tender, custardy crumb.
    - **Pan Prep:** Always brush metal 8.5 x 4.5-inch loaf pans with liquid vegetable oil rather than cooking spray so
      the cinnamon-sugar crust releases cleanly.
