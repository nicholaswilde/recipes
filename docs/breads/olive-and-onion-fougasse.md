# :bread: Olive and Onion Fougasse

![Olive and Onion Fougasse][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 1 loaf | 11 hours 12 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

=== "Poolish"

    - :bread: 1 cup (120 g) bread flour
    - :droplet: 1/2 cup (114 g) water
    - :tea: 1/8 tsp instant yeast

=== "Filling"

    - :butter: 1 Tbsp (14 g) [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
    - :onion: 1 cup (142 g) diced onion
    - :olive: 1/2 cup (71 g) pitted and roughly chopped Kalamata olives

=== "Dough"

    - :droplet: 1/2 cup (114 g) water
    - :bread: 1 cup (120 g) bread flour
    - :bread: 1 cup (113 g) whole wheat flour
    - :tea: 2 tsp instant yeast
    - :salt: 1 tsp table salt
    - :olive: 2 Tbsp (25 g) olive oil

## :cooking: Cookware

- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 bowl
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 pan
- :gear: 1 stand mixer
- :cookie: 1 baking sheet
- :knife: 1 knife
- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 oven
- :wastebasket: 1 cooling rack

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

To make the poolish, combine bread flour, water, and instant yeast in a bowl. Cover and set aside to rest overnight.

### Step 2

To prepare the filling, combine [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) and diced onion in a pan set over medium-low heat. Cook for about 20 minutes,
stirring every 5 minutes, until nicely caramelized. Transfer the onions to a bowl and cool to room temperature before
using.

### Step 3

To make the dough, add water, bread flour, and whole wheat flour to the poolish and mix until shaggy. Cover the bowl and
allow the mixture to rest for 20 minutes.

### Step 4

Add instant yeast, table salt, and olive oil, and knead the dough briefly until it forms a shaggy mass. Mix in the
[caramelized onions](../ingredients/caramelized-onions.md) and roughly chopped pitted Kalamata olives, and knead until fairly supple but not necessarily
elastic, about 3 minutes with a stand mixer or 5 minutes by hand.

### Step 5

Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl, cover, and allow it to rise at room temperature for 1 1/2 hours, gently
deflating it and turning it over after 45 minutes.

### Step 6

Shape the dough into a 12-inch by 6-inch oval, place it on a lightly greased baking sheet, and allow it to rise for
about 30 minutes.

### Step 7

Using a sharp knife or pair of scissors, cut a single slit all the way through the dough lengthwise down the center,
stopping about 1 inch from each end. Follow up with three or four diagonal slits on each side of the center slit.

### Step 8

To create a leaf-like appearance, open up each slit by gently stretching the dough with your fingers, leaving behind
holes that are at least 2 inches wide so the leaf design remains visible after rising.

### Step 9

Allow the fougasse to rise until noticeably puffy, 30 to 45 minutes.

### Step 10

Toward the end of the rising time, preheat the oven to 400°F. For an extra-crispy crust, place a baking stone on the
center rack.

### Step 11

Bake the fougasse for 20 minutes or until it turns golden brown. Remove from the oven and transfer to a cooling rack to
cool.

### Step 12

Store well-wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze for up to 1 month.
