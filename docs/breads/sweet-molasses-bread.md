# :bread: Sweet Molasses Bread

![Sweet Molasses Bread][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 4 | 2.75 hours |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :bread: 2.25 cups (270 g) all-purpose flour
- :bread: 1.75 cups (198 g) whole wheat flour
- :salt: 1.5 tsp salt
- :chocolate_bar: 2 Tbsp (14 g) cocoa powder
- :tea: 1.5 tsp (4 g) instant yeast
- :droplet: 1.25 cups (284 g) lukewarm water
- :olive: 2 Tbsp (25 g) vegetable oil
- :apple: 1/4 cup (78 g) honey or maple syrup
- :candy: 2 Tbsp (42 g) molasses
- :ear_of_rice: cornmeal, for dusting (optional)

## :cooking: Cookware

- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 large bowl
- :gear: 1 stand mixer
- :wood: 1 rolling pin
- :cookie: 1 baking sheet
- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 oven

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a large bowl, mix the dry ingredients: all-purpose flour, whole wheat flour, salt, cocoa powder, and instant yeast.

### Step 2

Add the wet ingredients: lukewarm water, vegetable oil, honey or maple syrup, and molasses. Mix until a dough forms.

### Step 3

Knead for 8 to 10 minutes by hand or in a stand mixer. When ready, the dough will feel tacky and smooth. If using a
stand mixer, the dough should stick to the bottom of the bowl but not to your fingers.

### Step 4

Cover the dough with plastic wrap or a damp towel and let it rise in a warm spot until doubled, about 90 minutes.

### Step 5

Deflate the dough. Flour your work surface and divide the dough into 4 pieces.

### Step 6

Using your hands or a rolling pin, flatten each piece into a rectangle. Roll into a cylinder and pinch the seam to
close.

### Step 7

Lightly dust a lined baking sheet with cornmeal (optional). Place the loaves on the sheet, leaving space between them as
they will grow.

### Step 8

Cover loosely with plastic wrap or a damp towel and let rise for 40 minutes, or until the loaves have almost doubled and
look puffy and light.

### Step 9

Preheat the oven to 350°F. Just before baking, lightly sprinkle the loaves with more cornmeal for texture, if desired.

### Step 10

Uncover the loaves and bake for 30 to 35 minutes.

### Step 11

Let the loaves cool for 20 minutes before slicing. Once cooled, store in an airtight container or sealed bag.
