# :potato: Hamburger Potato Buns

![Hamburger Potato Buns][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 6 | 2.83 hours |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :bread: 3 cups (360 g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- :potato: 0.25 cup (46 g) potato flour
- :glass_of_milk: 0.25 cup (28 g) nonfat dry milk
- :candy: 2 Tbsp (25 g) granulated sugar
- :salt: 1.25 tsp table salt
- :tea: 2 tsp (6 g) instant yeast
- :butter: 4 Tbsp (57 g) unsalted [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :droplet: 1.25 cups (284 g) lukewarm water
- :butter: 1 Tbsp (14 g) melted unsalted [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md)

## :cooking: Cookware

- :bowl_with_spoon: mixer
- :bowl_with_spoon: bowl
- :bowl_with_spoon: hamburger bun pan
- :cookie: baking sheet
- :shallow_pan_of_food: oven
- :wastebasket: wire rack

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Combine all of the dough ingredients: King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour (360 g), potato flour (46 g) (or 1/2 cup
(43 g) dried potato flakes), nonfat dry milk (28 g), granulated sugar (25 g), table salt (8 g), instant yeast, unsalted
[butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) (57 g) (at room temperature), and lukewarm water (284 g). Mix and knead them — by hand, mixer, or bread machine
— to make a soft dough.

### Step 2

Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm place for 1 hour, or until it's almost doubled
in bulk.

### Step 3

Turn the dough onto a lightly greased surface, gently deflate it, and divide it into 6 pieces. Roll each piece into a
ball.

### Step 4

Place the balls into the greased cups of a hamburger bun pan, flattening gently. Or place them on a lightly greased or
parchment-lined baking sheet, leaving about 2 " to 3 " between them; flatten gently.

### Step 5

Cover and let rise until the buns have doubled in size, 1 to 1.5 hours. Towards the end of the rising time, preheat the
oven to 350°F.

### Step 6

Bake the buns for 15 to 20 minutes, or until they're light golden brown.

### Step 7

Remove them from the oven, and brush them with melted unsalted [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) (14 g), if desired.

### Step 8

Transfer the buns to a wire rack to cool. Store buns, well-wrapped, at room temperature for several days; freeze for
longer storage.
