# :bread: Classic Chelsea Buns

![Classic Chelsea Buns][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 12 buns | 3 hours 50 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

### Dough

- :butter: 50 g (3 1/2 Tbsp) [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :glass_of_milk: 200 ml milk
- :bread: 500 g strong bread flour
- :tea: 7 g fast-action dried yeast
- :candy: 50 g caster sugar
- :salt: 1 tsp fine sea salt
- :tangerine: 1 lemon zest, finely grated
- :egg: 1 large egg, beaten

### Filling

- :butter: 50 g (3 1/2 Tbsp) [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md), softened
- :candy: 100 g light [brown sugar](../ingredients/brown-sugar.md)
- :chestnut: 2 tsp ground cinnamon
- :grapes: 200 g currants

### Glaze

- :candy: 50 g caster sugar
- :droplet: 3 Tbsp water

## :cooking: Cookware

- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 saucepan
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 large mixing bowl
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 small bowl
- :gear: 1 stand mixer
- :wood: 1 rolling pin
- :knife: 1 knife
- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 20x30cm baking tin
- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 oven
- :wastebasket: 1 wire rack

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Melt [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) in a saucepan over low heat, remove from the heat, and stir in milk to
take the chill off without making it too hot.

### Step 2

In a large mixing bowl (or the bowl of a stand mixer), combine strong bread flour, fast-action dried yeast, caster
sugar, fine sea salt, and finely grated lemon zest.

### Step 3

Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients and add beaten egg and the warm buttery milk mixture. Mix together
until a soft dough forms, adding a splash of extra milk if needed.

### Step 4

Knead for 10 minutes by hand on a lightly floured surface (or 5 minutes on low speed in a stand mixer fitted with the
dough hook) until the dough is smooth, springy, and elastic. Return the dough to the bowl, cover, and leave to rise in a
warm place for about 2 hours (or until doubled in size).

### Step 5

Knock the dough down, then roll it out on a lightly floured surface with a rolling pin to a rectangle measuring
approximately 34 cm by 48 cm (13 by 19 inches).

### Step 6

Spread softened [butter](../ingredients/butter/butter.md) evenly over the surface of the dough, leaving a 1-inch border
along one long edge.

### Step 7

In a small bowl, combine packed light [brown sugar](../ingredients/brown-sugar.md), ground cinnamon, and currants.
Scatter the spiced fruit mixture evenly over the buttered dough and press it in gently.

### Step 8

Dampen the clean border with a little water, then roll up the dough snugly from the opposite long edge into a tight log
towards the border, pinching the seam closed. Trim the uneven ends, then slice the log into 12 equal slices with a sharp
knife.

### Step 9

Grease and line a 20x30cm baking tin with baking parchment. Arrange the buns cut-side up in the tin, spaced about 1 cm
apart. Cover loosely and leave to prove in a warm place for about 45 minutes until puffy and touching.

### Step 10

Preheat the oven to 180 °C (350 °F / 160 °C fan). Bake the buns for 20 to 25 minutes until golden brown and cooked
through.

### Step 11

While the buns bake, dissolve caster sugar in 3 tablespoons water in a small saucepan and simmer for 1 minute, then
remove from the heat to cool.

### Step 12

Brush the warm glaze generously over the buns as soon as they come out of the oven to create a sticky, glossy finish.

### Step 13

Let the buns cool in the tin for at least 30 minutes on a wire rack before tearing apart and serving.
