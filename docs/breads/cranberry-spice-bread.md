# :bread: Cranberry Spice Bread

![Cranberry Spice Bread][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 16 | 60 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :bread: 2.5 cups (300 g) all-purpose flour
- :candy: 1.5 cups (234 g) sugar
- :chestnut: 2 tsp baking soda
- :chestnut: 2 tsp (6 g) ground cinnamon
- :salt: 0.5 tsp salt
- :glass_of_milk: 1.5 cups (340 g) milk
- :butter: 6 Tbsp margarine
- :takeout_box: 15 oz%can whole cranberry sauce

## :cooking: Cookware

- 2 loaf pans
- 1 large mixing bowl

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease two loaf pans.

### Step 2

In a large mixing bowl, whisk together all-purpose flour, sugar, baking soda, ground cinnamon, and salt.

### Step 3

Add milk, melted margarine, and whole cranberry sauce. Stir just until combined.

### Step 4

Divide batter evenly between the prepared loaf pans.

### Step 5

Bake at 350 °F for 1 hour until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
