# Chipotle Hummus Sandwich with Smoky Tempeh Bacon

![Chipotle Hummus Sandwich with Smoky Tempeh Bacon][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 1 | 0 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :hot_pepper: 3 Tbsp [Spicy Chipotle Hummus](../sauces-and-dressings/dips-and-spreads/spicy-chipotle-hummus.md)
- :bacon: 3 slices [Easy Tempeh Bacon](../breakfast/easy-tempeh-bacon.md)
- :bread: 2 slices sandwich bread of choice
- :cucumber: 6 slices fresh cucumber
- :tomato: 2 slices fresh tomato
- :avocado: 3 slices ripe avocado
- :leafy_green: 0.25 cup alfalfa sprouts

## :cooking: Cookware

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

If using homemade [Spicy Chipotle Hummus](../sauces-and-dressings/dips-and-spreads/spicy-chipotle-hummus.md) (or sub store-bought) and [Easy Tempeh Bacon](../breakfast/easy-tempeh-bacon.md), prepare them first. They can be made up to 3-4
days in advance. Otherwise, use store-bought tempeh bacon and cook according to package instructions.

### Step 2

Toast sandwich bread of choice (if desired) and slice the sandwich toppings: fresh cucumber, fresh tomato, and ripe
avocado.

### Step 3

Assemble sandwich by spreading [Spicy Chipotle Hummus](../sauces-and-dressings/dips-and-spreads/spicy-chipotle-hummus.md) on one slice of bread, then layer (in this order) fresh cucumber, [Easy Tempeh Bacon](../breakfast/easy-tempeh-bacon.md),
alfalfa sprouts (or sub greens of choice), fresh tomato, ripe avocado, and remaining slice of bread. Enjoy!

### Step 4

Best when fresh, but the hummus and tempeh bacon will keep stored separately for 3-4 days if you want to prep them ahead
and assemble sandwiches when ready for quick weekday lunches!
