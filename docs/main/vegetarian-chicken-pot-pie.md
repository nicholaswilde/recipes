# 🥧 Vegetarian Chicken Pot Pie

![Vegetarian Chicken Pot Pie][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 8 | 45 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :bread: 0.25 cup (30 g) all-purpose flour
- :herb: 2 tsp (3 g) poultry seasoning
- :salt: 0.5 tsp kosher salt
- :salt: 0.5 tsp (2 g) black pepper
- :poultry_leg: 2.5 lb vegetarian chicken pieces (cut into 1-inch pieces)
- :olive: 1 Tbsp (12 g) vegetable oil
- :olive: 1 Tbsp (12 g) vegetable oil
- :stew: 0.5 cup (99 g) vegetable broth
- :stew: 1.5 cups (297 g) vegetable broth
- :carrot: 1.5 cups (148 g) carrots (sliced)
- :leafy_green: 1.5 cups (213 g) celery (sliced)
- :onion: 1 cup (142 g) leeks or chopped onion (sliced)
- :mushroom: 1 cup (78 g) cremini mushrooms (sliced)
- :salt: 0.5 tsp kosher salt
- :beans: 1 cup (150 g) frozen peas
- :corn: 0.5 cup (76 g) frozen corn
- :herb: 2 Tbsp (21 g) fresh thyme leaves
- :bread: 0.25 cup (30 g) all-purpose flour
- :takeout_box: 1 cup (227 g) half-and-half
- :bread: 3 cups (360 g) all-purpose flour
- :salt: 1 tsp kosher salt
- :chestnut: 1 Tbsp baking powder
- :candy: 1 Tbsp (10 g) sugar
- :glass_of_milk: 0.75 cup (170 g) salted butter (cold, cut up)
- :baby_bottle: 1.25 cups (284 g) buttermilk

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 bowl
- 1 skillet
- 1 slow cooker

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Combine all-purpose flour, poultry seasoning, kosher salt, and black pepper in a large bowl. Add vegetarian chicken
pieces (cut into 1-inch pieces); toss to coat.

### Step 2

Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add half of the vegetarian chicken; cook 4 to 5 minutes or until
browned, stirring occasionally. Transfer to a 5- to 6-qt. slow cooker. Repeat with the remaining vegetable oil and the
remaining vegetarian chicken. Deglaze pan with vegetable broth, scraping up any browned bits; transfer to slow cooker.
Add the remaining vegetable broth, carrots (sliced), celery (sliced), leeks or chopped onion (sliced), cremini mushrooms
(sliced), and kosher salt to cooker.

### Step 3

Cover and cook on low 8 to 10 hours or on high 4 to 5 hours.

### Step 4

Stir in frozen peas, frozen corn, and fresh thyme leaves. In a bowl combine all-purpose flour and half-and-half; stir
into mixture in slow cooker. If cooking on low, turn slow cooker to high. Cover and cook 45 minutes more or until
thickened.

### Step 5

Meanwhile, for biscuits, preheat oven to 450°F. In a large bowl stir together the remaining all-purpose flour and
kosher salt, baking powder, and sugar. Using a pastry blender, cut in salted butter (cold, cut up) until it resembles
coarse crumbs. Add buttermilk. Using a fork, stir just until mixture is moistened. Turn dough out onto a lightly floured
surface. Knead dough just until it holds together. Place on a parchment paper-lined baking sheet. Pat dough into a
rectangle. Cut into eight squares. Separate squares slightly. If you like, brush with additional buttermilk and sprinkle
with additional pepper. Bake 14 to 16 minutes or until golden.

### Step 6

Top each serving of pot pie with a biscuit. If you like, garnish with additional thyme and pepper.
