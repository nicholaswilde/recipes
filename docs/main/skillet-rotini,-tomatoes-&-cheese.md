# :spaghetti: Skillet Rotini, Tomatoes & Cheese

![Skillet Rotini, Tomatoes & Cheese][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 6 | 2 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :cut_of_meat: 1 lb vegetarian Italian sausage
- :olive: 2 tsp (8 g) olive oil
- :onion: 5 green onions, cut into 1-inch diagonal pieces
- :spaghetti: 4 cups rotini [pasta](../ingredients/pasta.md)
- :tomato: 14.5 oz%can diced tomatoes
- :blue_circle: 15 oz%container [ricotta](../ingredients/ricotta.md) cheese
- :glass_of_milk: 0.25 cup (57 g) milk
- :herb: 0.25 cup (10 g) fresh basil, slivered
- :salt: salt
- :salt: pepper

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 large skillet

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a large skillet, sauté and crumble vegetarian Italian sausage in olive oil.

### Step 2

Add green onions, cut into 1-inch diagonal pieces and cooked rotini [pasta](../ingredients/pasta.md), sautéing until lightly golden.

### Step 3

Add drained diced tomatoes; cook 2 minutes.

### Step 4

Combine [ricotta](../ingredients/ricotta.md) cheese and milk; add to [pasta](../ingredients/pasta.md) mixture, mixing well until evenly coated.

### Step 5

Add fresh basil, slivered, salt, and pepper to taste. Cook until heated through.
