# :spaghetti: Cheesy Classic Lasagna

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 8 | 1.08 hours |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :cut_of_meat: 1 lb vegetarian Italian sausage
- :tea: 0.5 cup (48 g) onion
- :garlic: 1 garlic clove
- 16 oz [tomato sauce](../sauces-and-dressings/gravy-and-savory-sauces/tomato-sauce.md)
- 12 oz [tomato paste](../ingredients/tomato-paste.md)
- :droplet: 1.5 cups (340 g) hot water
- :herb: 1 tsp (2 g) dried basil leaves
- :herb: 1 tsp (2 g) dried oregano leaves
- :herb: 0.5 tsp (1 g) dried marjoram leaves
- :blue_circle: 15 oz (225 g) [ricotta](../ingredients/ricotta.md) cheese
- :cheese: 0.5 cup (50 g) Parmesan cheese
- 3 [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md)
- :salt: 0.5 tsp salt
- :spaghetti: 8 oz lasagna noodles
- :cheese: 2 cups (226 g) mozzarella cheese
- :cheese: 2 cups (454 g) Muenster cheese

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 medium skillet
- 1 medium bowl
- 1 9x13-inch baking dish

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 350°F.

### Step 2

In a medium skillet, combine vegetarian Italian sausage, chopped onion, and minced garlic clove. Cook over medium heat,
stirring often to break up sausage, until lightly browned; drain if necessary.

### Step 3

Reserve 2/3 cup of the [tomato sauce](../sauces-and-dressings/gravy-and-savory-sauces/tomato-sauce.md).

### Step 4

Stir remaining [tomato sauce](../sauces-and-dressings/gravy-and-savory-sauces/tomato-sauce.md), [tomato paste](../ingredients/tomato-paste.md), hot water, dried basil leaves, dried oregano leaves, and dried marjoram
leaves into the sausage mixture. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer for 5 minutes.

### Step 5

In a medium bowl, combine [ricotta](../ingredients/ricotta.md) cheese, grated Parmesan cheese, [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md), and salt; mix well.

### Step 6

In a 9x13-inch baking dish, spread the reserved 2/3 cup [tomato sauce](../sauces-and-dressings/gravy-and-savory-sauces/tomato-sauce.md).

### Step 7

Layer 1/3 of the uncooked lasagna noodles, 1/3 of the [ricotta](../ingredients/ricotta.md) mixture, 1/3 of the sausage [tomato sauce](../sauces-and-dressings/gravy-and-savory-sauces/tomato-sauce.md), and 1/3 of
shredded mozzarella cheese and shredded Muenster cheese.

### Step 8

Repeat layers twice more to create 3 full layers.

### Step 9

Bake uncovered at 350 °F for 40 to 50 minutes until hot and bubbly. Let stand for 10 minutes before slicing and
serving.
