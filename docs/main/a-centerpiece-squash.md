# :shallow_pan_of_food: A Centerpiece Squash

![A Centerpiece Squash][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 8 to 10 | 1 hour 10 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :melon: 1 large butternut squash
- :olive: 4 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- :salt: kosher salt
- :salt: freshly ground black pepper
- :droplet: 3/8 cup (85 g) water
- :garlic: 1 small shallot
- :candy: 3 tbsp packed light brown sugar
- :seedling: 2 tbsp [Dijon mustard](../sauces-and-dressings/dijon-mustard.md)
- :apple: 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- :chestnut: 1/4 cup (28 g) walnuts
- :cherries: 1/4 cup pomegranate seeds
- :herb: fresh chives

## :cooking: Cookware

- :knife: 1 vegetable peeler
- :spoon: 1 soup spoon
- :cookie: 1 rimmed baking sheet
- :chopsticks: 1 chopsticks
- :knife: 1 sharp knife
- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 small saucepan
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 whisk
- :spoon: 1 fish spatula
- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 serving platter

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat the oven to 425°F.

### Step 2

Use a vegetable peeler to peel butternut squash (about 4 lbs), continuing past the pale outer layer and any green
stripes until you reach the bright orange flesh. Cut the squash in half lengthwise, then use a soup spoon to scoop out
the seeds and stringy flesh.

### Step 3

On a rimmed baking sheet, rub the squash all over with extra-virgin olive oil and season both sides generously with
kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper. Turn the squash halves cut-side down and pour water onto the baking sheet.
Roast until the squash is slightly soft but still holds its shape, about 20 minutes. Remove from the oven and cool for
about 15 minutes, or until the baking sheet is comfortable to handle.

### Step 4

Place a pair of chopsticks (or two wooden spoon handles) along both sides of one squash half. Using a sharp knife, cut
crosswise into 1/4-inch-thick slices, letting the chopsticks stop you from cutting all the way through. Repeat with the
second squash half. Brush the top of the squash with extra-virgin olive oil and return to the oven. Roast until the
squash is completely tender and the tops are lightly caramelized, 30 to 35 minutes.

### Step 5

Meanwhile, in a small saucepan, heat the remaining extra-virgin olive oil over medium heat. Stir in shallot (finely
diced) and cook until softening, about 2 minutes. Add packed light brown sugar,
[Dijon mustard](../sauces-and-dressings/dijon-mustard.md), apple cider vinegar, and water. Using a whisk, whisk well to
combine, then let bubble while whisking occasionally until the bubbles turn thick and slow, 3 to 4 minutes. Remove from
the heat and whisk again until glossy and syrupy (return to heat for 1 to 2 minutes if needed). Taste and season with
salt and pepper.

### Step 6

Slide a fish spatula (or a long offset spatula or long chef's knife) under the squash to release it cleanly from the
baking sheet. Transfer the squash halves to a serving platter, then spoon the warm glaze over the top so it slides down
the sides. Sprinkle with walnuts (toasted and finely chopped), pomegranate seeds, and fresh chives (thinly sliced) just
before serving.
