# :jack_o_lantern: Pumpkin Risotto

![Pumpkin Risotto][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 4 | 22 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- 3.5 cups [vegetable broth](../ingredients/vegetable-broth.md)
- :olive: 4 tsp (17 g) olive oil
- :tea: 1 onion
- :garlic: 1 garlic clove
- :ear_of_rice: 1 cup (200 g) Arborio rice
- :wine_glass: 1 cup (106 g) dry white wine
- :melon: 1 cup (227 g) pumpkin puree
- :cheese: 0.25 cup (25 g) Parmesan cheese
- 1 Tbsp [brown sugar](../ingredients/brown-sugar.md)
- :chestnut: 0.25 tsp (1 g) ground cinnamon
- :salt: 0.25 tsp salt
- :chestnut: 0.12 tsp ground nutmeg

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 saucepan
- 1 large saucepan

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a saucepan, bring [vegetable broth](../ingredients/vegetable-broth.md) to a boil, then reduce heat to keep at a gentle simmer.

### Step 2

In a separate large saucepan, heat olive oil. Sauté chopped onion and minced garlic clove until softened.

### Step 3

Add Arborio rice and stir until translucent around edges, about 1 to 2 minutes.

### Step 4

Add dry white wine and stir until absorbed.

### Step 5

Add simmering [vegetable broth](../ingredients/vegetable-broth.md), about 1/2 cup at a time, stirring frequently until liquid is absorbed before adding more,
about 18 to 20 minutes.

### Step 6

Stir in pumpkin puree, grated Parmesan cheese, [brown sugar](../ingredients/brown-sugar.md), ground cinnamon, and salt.

### Step 7

Sprinkle with ground nutmeg and serve warm.
