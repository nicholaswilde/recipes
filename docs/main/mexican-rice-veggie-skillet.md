# :rice: Mexican Rice Veggie Skillet

![Mexican Rice Veggie Skillet][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 4 | 20 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :beans: 15 oz%can kidney beans
- :tomato: 15 oz%can chopped tomatoes
- 10 oz%jar [enchilada sauce](../sauces-and-dressings/gravy-and-savory-sauces/enchilada-sauce.md)
- :droplet: 1 cup (227 g) water
- :carrot: 2 cups (304 g) frozen mixed vegetables
- :candy: 1.5 cups (129 g) instant [brown rice](../ingredients/brown-rice.md)

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 large skillet

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a large skillet, combine rinsed and drained kidney beans, chopped tomatoes, [enchilada sauce](../sauces-and-dressings/gravy-and-savory-sauces/enchilada-sauce.md), water, and frozen mixed
vegetables.

### Step 2

Bring mixture to a boil over medium-high heat.

### Step 3

Stir in uncooked instant [brown rice](../ingredients/brown-rice.md).

### Step 4

Reduce heat to low; cover and simmer for 18 to 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until rice is tender and liquid is
absorbed.
