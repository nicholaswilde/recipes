# :stuffed_flatbread: Veggie Enchiladas

![Veggie Enchiladas][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 6 | 36 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :droplet: 2 tsp (9 g) canola oil
- :mushroom: 8 oz (78 g) portobello mushrooms
- :mushroom: 1 cup (106 g) white mushrooms
- :cucumber: 1 zucchini
- :tea: 4 scallions
- :garlic: 2 garlic cloves
- :salt: 1 tsp (2 g) adobo seasoning
- 19 oz%can [enchilada sauce](../sauces-and-dressings/gravy-and-savory-sauces/enchilada-sauce.md)
- :flatbread: 6 8-inch fat-free [flour tortillas](../breads/flour-tortillas.md)
- :glass_of_milk: 6 Tbsp (85 g) fat-free sour cream
- :cheese: 0.5 cup (57 g) reduced-fat cheddar cheese
- :apple: 0.5 cup (71 g) black olives

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 9x13-inch baking dish
- 1 large skillet

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 375°F. Spray a 9x13-inch baking dish with cooking spray.

### Step 2

Heat canola oil in a large skillet. Add chopped portobello mushrooms, chopped white mushrooms, diced zucchini, thinly
sliced scallions, minced garlic cloves, and adobo seasoning. Cook, stirring occasionally, until liquid has evaporated,
about 6 minutes.

### Step 3

Stir in 1/4 cup of the [enchilada sauce](../sauces-and-dressings/gravy-and-savory-sauces/enchilada-sauce.md) and heat through.

### Step 4

Warm fat-free [flour tortillas](../breads/flour-tortillas.md). Top each tortilla with 1/6 of the mushroom mixture, then with 1 Tbsp fat-free sour cream.
Roll up and place seam-side down in the baking dish.

### Step 5

Spoon remaining [enchilada sauce](../sauces-and-dressings/gravy-and-savory-sauces/enchilada-sauce.md) over top. Sprinkle with shredded reduced-fat cheddar cheese and sliced black olives.

### Step 6

Cover and bake for 15 minutes. Uncover and bake for about 10 minutes more. Let stand for 5 minutes before serving.
