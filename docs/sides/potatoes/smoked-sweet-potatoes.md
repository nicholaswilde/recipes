# :sweet_potato: Smoked Sweet Potatoes

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 4 | 2.50 hours |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :sweet_potato: 4 sweet potatoes

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 fork
- 1 smoker

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Prep the potatoes: Wash and dry sweet potatoes thoroughly, then pierce each several times with a fork so steam can
escape. Skip the oil.

### Step 2

Set up the smoker: Preheat the smoker to 225°F - 250°F. Use mild woods like apple, cherry, or pecan.

### Step 3

Smoke low and slow: Place directly on the smoker grates for 2 to 2.5 hours (up to 3 hours for extra-large potatoes).

### Step 4

Check for doneness: They are ready when skins are slightly wrinkled and a fork glides through the thickest center with
zero resistance.
