# :potato: Crispy BBQ Sweet Potatoes

![Crispy BBQ Sweet Potatoes][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 4 | 8 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :tomato: 3 Tbsp ketchup
- :takeout_box: 1 Tbsp (14 g) vegetarian Worcestershire sauce
- :wine_glass: 1 Tbsp (13 g) red wine vinegar
- :takeout_box: 1 tsp (3 g) prepared mustard
- :salt: 0.5 tsp (2 g) black pepper
- :sweet_potato: 1 lb sweet potatoes

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 grill grates
- 1 small bowl

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Spray grill grates with nonstick spray; prepare grill for medium-hot fire.

### Step 2

In a small bowl, combine ketchup, vegetarian Worcestershire sauce, red wine vinegar, prepared mustard, and black pepper.

### Step 3

Brush mixture onto peeled and cut sweet potatoes (sliced into 1/4-inch slices).

### Step 4

Grill sweet potato slices, turning frequently and brushing with remaining ketchup mixture until crispy and tender, about
8 minutes.
