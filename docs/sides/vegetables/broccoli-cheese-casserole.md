# :broccoli: Broccoli Cheese Casserole

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 6 | 45 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :broccoli: 10 oz%pkg frozen broccoli florets
- :corn: 15 oz%can creamed corn
- :egg: 0.25 cup (30 g) fat-free egg substitute
- :tea: 0.33 cup (32 g) onion
- :cheese: 0.75 cup (86 g) reduced-fat cheddar cheese
- :salt: salt
- :salt: black pepper

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 2-qt casserole dish
- 1 bowl

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a 2-qt casserole dish with cooking spray.

### Step 2

Cook frozen broccoli florets according to package directions.

### Step 3

In a bowl, mix cooked broccoli, creamed corn, fat-free egg substitute, chopped onion, grated reduced-fat cheddar cheese,
salt, and black pepper to taste.

### Step 4

Transfer mixture into the casserole dish. Cover and bake for 45 minutes.
