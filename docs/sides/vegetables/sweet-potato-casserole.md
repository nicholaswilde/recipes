# :sweet_potato: Sweet Potato Casserole

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 12 | 1.42 hours |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :sweet_potato: 3 lbs sweet potatoes
- :tangerine: 0.5 cup (140 g) + 1%Tbsp orange juice
- :wine_glass: 3 Tbsp brandy
- :tangerine: 1 tsp (2 g) grated orange rind
- :butter: 1 tsp reduced-calorie margarine
- :sweet_potato: 0.25 tsp (1 g) ground ginger
- :salt: salt
- :salt: black pepper
- :egg: 3 egg whites
- 0.5 cup packed [brown sugar](../../ingredients/brown-sugar.md)
- :chestnut: 0.33 cup (38 g) pecans
- :bread: 3 Tbsp (22 g) all-purpose flour
- :chestnut: 1 tsp (3 g) ground cinnamon
- :apple: 1.25 cups (350 g) apple

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 large pot
- 1 clean bowl
- 1 11x7-inch baking dish
- 1 small bowl

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Place sweet potatoes in a large pot covered in boiling water. Cook covered for 35 to 40 minutes until tender. Drain and
let cool. Peel and mash sweet potatoes.

### Step 2

To the mashed sweet potatoes, add 1/2 cup of the orange juice, brandy, grated orange rind, reduced-calorie margarine,
ground ginger, salt, and black pepper to taste; mix well.

### Step 3

In a clean bowl, beat egg whites at high speed until soft peaks form. Gradually add 1/4 cup of the packed [brown sugar](../../ingredients/brown-sugar.md), 1
Tbsp at a time, beating until stiff peaks form.

### Step 4

Gently stir 1/4 of the egg white mixture into the sweet potato mixture. Gently fold in the remaining egg white mixture.

### Step 5

Spoon mixture into an 11x7-inch baking dish sprayed with cooking spray; set aside.

### Step 6

Preheat oven to 350°F.

### Step 7

In a small bowl, combine remaining 1/4 cup [brown sugar](../../ingredients/brown-sugar.md), chopped pecans, all-purpose flour, and ground cinnamon; stir
well.

### Step 8

Stir finely chopped apple and remaining 1 Tbsp orange juice into the pecan mixture.

### Step 9

Sprinkle topping evenly over the sweet potato mixture.

### Step 10

Bake at 350 °F for 30 to 35 minutes. Let stand 10 minutes prior to serving.
