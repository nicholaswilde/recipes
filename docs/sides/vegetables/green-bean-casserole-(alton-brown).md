# :beans: Green Bean Casserole (Alton Brown)

![Green Bean Casserole (Alton Brown)](../../assets/images/green-bean-casserole-(alton-brown).webp){ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 6 to 8 | 1 hour 10 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :droplet: 3 quarts water
- :salt: 2 Tbsp plus 2 tsp kosher salt, divided, plus more to taste
- :beans: 1 lb (454 g) fresh green beans, trimmed and halved
- :avocado: Neutral oil, for frying
- :onion: 2 large onions, thinly sliced on a mandoline
- :bread: 1/4 cup (36 g) [rice flour](../../ingredients/rice-flour.md)
- :butter: 5 Tbsp (71 g) unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md)
- :mushroom: 16 oz (454 g) cremini mushrooms, quartered
- :salt: 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- :garlic: 4 cloves garlic, minced
- :chestnut: 1 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
- :bread: 1/3 cup (40 g) all-purpose flour
- :takeout_box: 1 tsp soy sauce powder
- :takeout_box: 1 Tbsp sherry vinegar
- :stew: 2 cups (454 g) unsalted [vegetable broth](../../ingredients/vegetable-broth.md)
- :glass_of_milk: 2 cups (454 g) half and half

## :cooking: Cookware

- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 Dutch oven
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 salad spinner
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 half sheet pan
- :knife: 1 mandoline
- :bowl_with_spoon: 1 medium bowl
- :wastebasket: 1 spider strainer
- :shallow_pan_of_food: 1 12-inch cast-iron skillet

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Heat oven to 350°F with a rack in the middle.

### Step 2

Bring water and 2 Tbsp of the kosher salt to a boil in a large, heavy pot, such as a Dutch oven. Prepare an ice bath in
a salad spinner. Add fresh green beans to the boiling water, return to a boil, and cook until just tender, about 8
minutes. Drain the beans, then immediately transfer to the ice bath. Let sit until cool, about 5 minutes, then remove
the basket insert and pour out the ice water. Spin the green beans to dry them.

### Step 3

Pour 1 inch of neutral oil into a large heavy pot (or clean the one you used for the beans), and bring to 375°F over
medium-high heat. Line a half sheet pan with paper towels and set next to the cooktop.

### Step 4

While the oil heats, thinly slice large onions on a mandoline, then combine the onions and
[rice flour](../../ingredients/rice-flour.md) in a medium bowl and toss to coat the slices evenly in the flour. When the
oil reaches 375°F, carefully add about a quarter of the onion slices to the oil and fry, stirring occasionally, until
golden brown, about 5 minutes. Use a spider strainer to transfer to the prepared sheet pan and season lightly with salt.
Repeat with the remaining onions, then set aside.

### Step 5

Melt unsalted [butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) in a 12-inch cast-iron skillet over medium heat. When the
[butter](../../ingredients/butter/butter.md) is foamy, add cremini mushrooms, the remaining 2 tsp kosher salt, and
freshly ground black pepper. Cook, stirring frequently, until the mushrooms have given off all of their liquid, the
liquid has evaporated, and the mushrooms have browned, about 10 minutes.

### Step 6

Add garlic and freshly grated nutmeg and cook, stirring constantly, until aromatic, about 30 seconds. Sift all-purpose
flour and soy sauce powder over the mushrooms, then continue to cook, stirring, until the rawness has been cooked out of
the flour, about 1 minute.

### Step 7

Stir in sherry vinegar and cook about 1 minute. Slowly stir in unsalted
[vegetable broth](../../ingredients/vegetable-broth.md), scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the skillet.
Bring to a simmer, then stir in half and half. Return to a simmer, then reduce the heat to low. Continue to simmer,
stirring frequently, until the mixture is quite thick, about 12 minutes. Remove from the heat.

### Step 8

Stir in the green beans and half of the fried onions, then top with the remaining onions. Transfer to the oven and bake
until the sauce is bubbly around the edges and the onions are deeply browned, about 10 minutes.

!!! tip "Make-Ahead"

    - The fried onions can be made up to 1 week ahead and stored in an airtight container at room temperature.
    - The green bean casserole can be assembled up to 1 day ahead. Leave off the top layer of fried onions until ready
      to bake, and bring the casserole out of the refrigerator about 1 hour before baking.
