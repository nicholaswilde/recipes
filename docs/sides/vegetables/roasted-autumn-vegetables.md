# :carrot: Roasted Autumn Vegetables

![Roasted Autumn Vegetables][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 6 | 60 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :carrot: 6 baby carrots
- :potato: 3 small potatoes, cut into 1-inch pieces
- :carrot: 3 small parsnips, cut into 1-inch pieces
- :sweet_potato: 1 large sweet potato, cut into 1-inch pieces
- :onion: 2 large onions, halved crosswise with peel and roots
- :bell_pepper: 2 sweet bell peppers
- :garlic: 6 garlic cloves
- 0.5 cup [vegetable broth](../../ingredients/vegetable-broth.md)
- :olive: 2 Tbsp (25 g) olive oil
- :salt: salt
- :salt: black pepper
- :takeout_box: 2 Tbsp balsamic vinegar
- :herb: 3 sprigs fresh rosemary

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 large shallow roasting pan

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 450°F.

### Step 2

In a large shallow roasting pan, combine halved baby carrots, potatoes, cut into 1-inch pieces, peeled and cut parsnips,
cut into 1-inch pieces, peeled and cut sweet potato, cut into 1-inch pieces, onions, halved crosswise with peel and
roots, halved and seeded sweet bell peppers, and unpeeled garlic cloves.

### Step 3

Drizzle vegetable mixture with [vegetable broth](../../ingredients/vegetable-broth.md) and olive oil; season with salt and black pepper.

### Step 4

Roast uncovered at 450 °F for 45 minutes, stirring vegetables occasionally.

### Step 5

Stir in balsamic vinegar and fresh rosemary. Roast for 15 minutes more, or until vegetables are tender and caramelized.
