# Gurkensalat (German Cucumber Salad)

![Gurkensalat (German Cucumber Salad)][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 4 | 15 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :apple: 2 large English cucumbers
- :salt: 0.25 tsp salt
- :glass_of_milk: 0.25 cup (57 g) light sour cream
- :glass_of_milk: 3 Tbsp (43 g) low-fat plain strained yogurt
- :takeout_box: 1.5 Tbsp (32 g) cider vinegar
- :apple: 1 Tbsp (11 g) chopped fresh dill
- :salt: 0.25 tsp salt
- :hot_pepper: 0.5 tsp (2 g) ground pepper

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 serving bowl

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Using a mandoline, thinly slice English cucumbers (unpeeled). Arrange the cucumber slices in a single layer on paper
towels or a clean kitchen towel. Sprinkle with salt; let stand until the cucumbers release some of their moisture and
look glossy, about 15 minutes.

### Step 2

Meanwhile, whisk light sour cream, low-fat plain strained yogurt (Greek-style), cider vinegar, chopped fresh dill, the
remaining salt, and ground pepper together in a large serving bowl until smooth. Refrigerate until ready to use.

### Step 3

Pat the cucumbers dry with clean paper towels or a clean kitchen towel. Add the cucumbers to the dressing and toss to
coat evenly. Garnish with additional dill and pepper, if desired. Serve immediately.
