# :leafy_green: Grilled Romaine Lettuce

![Grilled Romaine Lettuce][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 4 | 4 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :olive: 0.5 cup (100 g) olive oil
- :takeout_box: 0.25 cup balsamic vinegar
- :apple: 2 Tbsp (42 g) agave
- :hot_pepper: 0.5 tsp (1 g) fresh cracked pepper
- :leafy_green: 2 heads romaine lettuce
- :olive: 1 olive oil
- :salt: 1 salt
- :salt: 1 pepper
- :cheese: 0.5 cup (50 g) freshly grated Parmesan cheese

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 small bowl
- 1 refrigerator
- 1 grill
- 1 large bowl

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

In a small bowl, whisk olive oil, balsamic vinegar, agave, and fresh cracked pepper together for
the [vinaigrette](../../sauces-and-dressings/vinaigrettes/vinaigrette.md). Store in the refrigerator until ready to use.

### Step 2

Fire up the grill to 400°F and higher (or Weber Smokey Mountain cooker set up for direct grilling, one zone).

### Step 3

Lightly drizzle the cut side of romaine lettuce (washed and halved lengthwise) with olive oil, then season with salt and
pepper to taste.

### Step 4

When grilling the romaine lettuce, the grill should be 400°F and higher. Place the romaine halves onto the grill cut side
down. Grill checking the underside frequently until a nice char forms, about 1 to 2 minutes. Flip and grill the other
side for 1 to 2 minutes.

### Step 5

Remove from the grill and sprinkle freshly grated Parmesan cheese over each half on the cut side, then drizzle with the
balsamic [vinaigrette](../../sauces-and-dressings/vinaigrettes/vinaigrette.md). Alternatively, chop the grilled lettuce, transfer to a large bowl, and toss with the Parmesan
cheese and balsamic [vinaigrette](../../sauces-and-dressings/vinaigrettes/vinaigrette.md) before serving.
