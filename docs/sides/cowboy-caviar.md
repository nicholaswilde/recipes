# Cowboy Caviar

![Cowboy Caviar][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:------------------------:|
| 8 cups | 15 minutes, plus 2 hours chilling |

## :salt: Ingredients

### Dressing

- :olive: ¼ cup olive oil
- :wine_glass: ¼ cup red wine vinegar
- :garlic: 3–4 garlic cloves, minced
- :sugar: 1 tsp granulated sugar
- :salt: kosher salt and black pepper, to taste

### Salad

- :tomato: 3 plum tomatoes, cored and diced
- :onion: ¼ cup red onion, finely diced
- :beans: 1 (15-oz) can black beans, rinsed
- :beans: 1 (15-oz) can black-eyed peas, rinsed
- :corn: 1½ cups corn kernels (fresh from 2–4 cobs, or thawed frozen)
- :bell_pepper: 1 bell pepper, seeded and finely diced (red, green, or yellow)
- :hot_pepper: 1 jalapeño, seeded and finely diced
- :herb: ⅓ cup cilantro leaves and tender stems, chopped
- :onion: 1 scallion, chopped (optional, for garnish)
- :chip: tortilla chips, for serving

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 medium bowl
- 1 whisk

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Make the dressing: In a medium bowl, whisk together olive oil, red wine vinegar, garlic, sugar, ½ tsp salt, and ½ tsp pepper.

### Step 2

Add tomatoes, red onion, black beans, black-eyed peas, corn, bell pepper, jalapeño, and cilantro. Toss to combine and season with salt and pepper to taste. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving.

### Step 3

To serve, toss well and season to taste. Sprinkle with scallions if using, and serve with tortilla chips.
