# Bread and Butter Pickles

![Bread and Butter Pickles](../assets/images/bread-and-butter-pickles.webp){ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 16 | 1.10 days |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :apple: 1.5 lbs pickling cucumbers sliced 1/4-inch thick (which is about 5.5 cups)
- :apple: 1.5 Tbsp coarse salt
- :tea: 1 cup (240 g) thinly sliced sweet onion
- :candy: 1 cup (198 g) granulated sugar
- :takeout_box: 1 cup (106 g) white vinegar
- :apple: 0.5 cup (140 g) apple cider vinegar
- :candy: 0.25 cup (53 g) light brown sugar
- :seedling: 1.5 tsp mustard seeds
- :chestnut: 0.5 tsp (1 g) celery seeds
- :curry: 0.12 tsp ground turmeric

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 bowl
- 1 colander
- 1 bowl
- 1 bowl
- 1 medium saucepan
- 1 airtight container

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Combine pickling cucumbers sliced 1/4-inch thick (which is about 5.5 cups) and coarse salt in a large, shallow bowl;
cover and chill 1.5 hours.

### Step 2

Move cucumbers into a colander and rinse thoroughly under cold water. Drain well, and return cucumbers to bowl.

### Step 3

Add thinly sliced sweet onion to the bowl and toss with the cucumbers.

### Step 4

Combine the granulated sugar, white vinegar, apple cider vinegar, light brown sugar, mustard seeds, celery seeds and
ground turmeric in a medium saucepan; bring to a simmer over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves.

### Step 5

Pour the hot vinegar mixture over cucumber mixture; let stand at room temperature 1 hour.

### Step 6

Cover and refrigerate 24 hours. (If you’re really impatient, though, they taste great after only a few hours!)

### Step 7

Store in an airtight container in refrigerator up to 1 month.
