# :cucumber: 1/2 Sour Pickles

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 1 | 2-3 days |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :cucumber: 1-2 lb cucumbers, washed
- :droplet: water (see instructions)
- :salt: salt (see instructions)

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 mason jar
- 1 electronic kitchen scale

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Cut the washed cucumber how you want. I choose to keep them whole for this recipe. 

### Step 2

To a clean mason jar, pack in the cucumbers. 

### Step 3

Get an electronic kitchen scale, place the jar filled with cucumbers on, and tare the scale so that it weights zero. 

### Step 4

Pour water all the way to the top. Get the weight of the water in grams. 

### Step 5

Take that number and multiply it by 0.04. This will tell you the correct amount of salt you need in grams. 

### Step 6

Tare the scale so it reads zero and pour the predetermined amount of salt into the jar. Seal the jar and shake so the salt will dissolve and disburse through out the jar. 

### Step 7

Label the jar and let ferment for 1-2 days at room temperature. After they are done fermenting, place in the fridge and enjoy!
