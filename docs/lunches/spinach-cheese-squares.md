# :cheese_wedge: Spinach Cheese Squares

![Spinach Cheese Squares][1]{ loading=lazy }

| :fork_and_knife_with_plate: Serves | :timer_clock: Total Time |
|:----------------------------------:|:-----------------------: |
| 12 | 45 minutes |

## :salt: Ingredients

- :butter: 4 Tbsp margarine
- 3 [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md)
- :bread: 1 cup (120 g) all-purpose flour
- :glass_of_milk: 1 cup (227 g) milk
- :salt: 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md)
- :cheese: 1 lb sharp cheddar cheese
- :leafy_green: 20 oz (380 g) frozen chopped spinach

## :cooking: Cookware

- 1 9x13-inch baking pan
- 1 large bowl

## :pencil: Instructions

### Step 1

Preheat oven to 350°F. Place margarine in a 9x13-inch baking pan and melt in the oven.

### Step 2

In a large bowl, whisk together [eggs](../ingredients/eggs.md), all-purpose flour, milk, salt, and [baking powder](../ingredients/baking-powder.md).

### Step 3

Stir in shredded sharp cheddar cheese and thawed, drained frozen chopped spinach.

### Step 4

Pour mixture into the prepared pan. Bake for 35 minutes.

### Step 5

Remove and allow to cool for 10 minutes before cutting into squares.
